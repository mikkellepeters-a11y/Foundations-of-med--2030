Hemolysis signature: anemia + retic up, LDH up, indirect bilirubin up, haptoglobin down.

Intravascular

  • RBC bursts in vessel
  • Hemoglobinemia / hemoglobinuria
  • Hemosiderinuria later
  • Very low haptoglobin
  • Mechanical shear, complement, toxin/infection

Extravascular

  • Spleen/liver macrophage clearance
  • Splenomegaly
  • Indirect bilirubin / jaundice
  • Spherocytes common
  • HS, warm AIHA

Hereditary spherocytosis

  • Spectrin/ankyrin/band 3-type membrane defect
  • Loss of surface area -> spherocyte -> splenic trapping
  • MCHC high
  • Osmotic fragility increased (slide)
  • Splenectomy reduces hemolysis; pigment gallstones

G6PD deficiency

  • X-linked pattern
  • Low NADPH -> low reduced glutathione -> oxidative injury
  • Triggers: infection, oxidant meds, fava beans
  • Heinz bodies -> bite cells
  • G6PD level may be falsely normal during attack

MAHA, TTP & Transfusion Reactions

Emergency rule: schistocytes + thrombocytopenia is not a "review later" finding. Evaluate TMA/TTP immediately.

TTP

FactoidMust know
MechanismSevere ADAMTS13 deficiency -> ultralarge vWF multimers -> platelet-rich microthrombi -> RBC shear
Labs/smearMAHA + schistocytes + thrombocytopenia; PT/aPTT often normal
SymptomsNeuro symptoms common; renal findings can occur; fever possible
PLASMIC6-7 high probability; 5 intermediate; 0-4 low in slide
Critical actionDo NOT wait for ADAMTS13. Start plasma exchange when strongly suspected.
Lecture treatmentPLEX + steroids + rituximab +/- caplacizumab

TTP vs DIC

TTP: platelet-vWF microthrombi; PT/aPTT often normal.

DIC: coagulation-factor consumption; PT/aPTT often prolonged, fibrinogen low, D-dimer high.

Coombs

Direct DAT

Are Ig/complement already attached to the patient's RBCs? Use for AIHA/transfusion-reaction evaluation.

Indirect

Are anti-RBC antibodies floating in serum? Used in pretransfusion antibody screening.

Acute transfusion-reaction first move: STOP the transfusion. Preserve IV access, verify identity, notify the blood bank, and evaluate for hemolysis.

If you see X -> think Y

  • Spherocytes + DAT negative + family hx -> HS
  • Spherocytes + DAT positive -> warm AIHA
  • Bite cells after TMP-SMX/fava/infection -> G6PD
  • Acrocyanosis after cold/Mycoplasma -> cold agglutinin disease
  • Schistocytes + Plt 20K + confusion -> TTP
  • Normal PT/aPTT with MAHA -> supports TTP over DIC
  • Dark urine + hypotension during transfusion -> acute HTR
  • Jaundice + falling Hb days later -> delayed HTR
Last 5 minutes: retic/LDH/bili up, haptoglobin down. HS = spherocyte, high MCHC, DAT-. Warm = IgG/DAT+. Cold = IgM/C3. G6PD = Heinz -> bite cells; test can be false-normal during crisis. TTP = ADAMTS13, schistocytes + low platelets, PLEX now.
