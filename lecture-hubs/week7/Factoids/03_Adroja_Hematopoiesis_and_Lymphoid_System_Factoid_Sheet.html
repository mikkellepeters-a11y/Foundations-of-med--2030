Histology + Traffic Factoids

Lymph node

Outside in: capsule -> subcapsular sinus -> cortex (B) -> paracortex (T + HEV) -> medulla -> hilum.
ZoneWhat lives there
CortexB-cell follicles / germinal centers
ParacortexT cells + HEVs
Medullary cordsPlasma cells
Medullary sinusesMacrophages + lymph flow
Two routes in: afferent lymphatics bring antigen/DCs; blood brings naive B/T through HEVs. One route out: efferent lymphatic at hilum.

Spleen

ZoneMemorize
Red pulpCords of Billroth + sinusoids; tests/removes old RBCs
PALST-cell zone around central arteriole
FollicleB-cell zone
Marginal zoneMacrophages + memory B; first blood-borne antigen contact
Spleen filters BLOOD. Open circulation: artery -> cords -> squeeze through sinus slits -> venous return. No spleen -> Howell-Jolly bodies + encapsulated-organism risk.

Thymus

  • Anterior mediastinum
  • Epithelium: 3rd pharyngeal pouch
  • Dark cortex, pale medulla
  • Hassall corpuscles = medulla
  • No follicles/germinal centers
  • Positive selection = cortex: can see self-MHC?
  • Negative selection = medulla: binds self too strongly?
  • AIRE helps display tissue antigens
  • Exit as CD4 or CD8 single-positive cells
