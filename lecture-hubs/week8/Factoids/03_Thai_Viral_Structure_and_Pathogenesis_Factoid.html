DNA vs RNA Rules

DNA
Usually nucleus. Pox is classic cytoplasmic exception. HBV is special because it uses an RNA intermediate and reverse transcriptase.
RNA
Usually cytoplasm. Influenza and retroviruses are nuclear exceptions. +RNA can act as mRNA; -RNA requires viral RdRp.
Influenza: 8 segments. Drift = slow change/yearly outbreaks. Shift = reassortment/pandemic potential.

Pathogenesis + Histopathology

Transmission / Progression

  • Portal: broken skin or mucosa; inhalation examples include influenza and SARS-CoV-2.
  • Bloodstream spread = viremia.
  • Acquisition → incubation/amplification → prodrome → tissue-specific disease → immune response → shedding → resolution/persistence.
