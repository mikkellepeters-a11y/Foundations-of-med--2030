Mpox

Deck phenotype
Orthopoxvirus, not herpes. Direct skin-to-skin contact with lesions; often genital in U.S. cases; painful vesicles at inoculation site → crust.
Dx / prevention
Lesion PCR. JYNNEOS = non-replicating modified vaccinia vaccine, 2 doses, for exposed or at-risk people.
Source conflict: older MCG 2029 handout says tecovirimat may be used in severe Mpox. Current 2026 deck says no known therapy and tecovirimat failed in Mpox trials. Follow the current deck.
