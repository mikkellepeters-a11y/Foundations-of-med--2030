Warning Signs + First-Line Screen

Child red flags
Family history • abnormal TREC • ≥4 ear/yr • ≥2 serious sinus/yr • ≥2 pneumonias/yr • deep abscess/sepsis • persistent thrush/fungal skin disease • ≥2 months antibiotics with poor response • failure to thrive • IV antibiotics.
First line
CBC + diff • quantitative Ig levels GAME • maybe delayed-hypersensitivity skin testing • HIV-1 testing • C3/C4/CH50/AH50 • review newborn TREC.
ScreenWhat to extract
CBC/diffAbsolute lymphocyte + neutrophil counts; platelet number + size; smear. Low ALC → SCID concern. Microthrombocytopenia → WAS clue.
GAMEAll near-zero → SCID; ≥2 low classes/no explanation → “CVID” phenotype; high/normal IgM + other classes very low → hyper-IgM.
TRECCongenital lymphopenia screen, not SCID-specific. SCID, DiGeorge, A-T, syndromes, and secondary causes can lower it.
ComplementCH50 classical; AH50 alternative; add C3/C4. C1 inhibitor quantity/function for angioedema; C1q for acquired angioedema.

Named IEIs - Lecture Anchors

IEILecture cue
“CVID”Working phenotype: ≥2 low Ig classes + no alternate explanation. BAFF/TACI/APRIL high-yield. Genotype when possible. Gamma-globulin replacement; APDS may be targeted with leniolisib.
XLAAntibody-deficiency phenotype; very low Ig; BTK is model targeted gene test. Companion: absent B cells / boy after maternal IgG wanes.

Stealth Objective

Recognize, don't over-memorize: ataxia-telangiectasia • MSMD • hyper-IgE syndrome(s) • CMC / APS-1 • IRAK4/MyD88 deficiency • UNC93B/TLR3 deficiency.
Exam logic: broad opportunists + low ALC = combined • bacterial = antibody (but check complement/phagocyte mimics) • Neisseria = terminal complement • deep catalase-type abscess pattern = phagocyte • T/B/NK pattern = derive from development.
