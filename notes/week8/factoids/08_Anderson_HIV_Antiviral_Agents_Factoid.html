HIV Antiviral Agents - Factoid Sheet

Week 8 • Core Lecture 8 • Anderson deck controls
Life-cycle map: entry → reverse transcription → integration → assembly/maturation. Match the class to the step before memorizing the drug.

ART Goals + Starting Considerations

Goals
Undetectable viral load • no replication = less mutation • U=U • CD4 recovery • fewer opportunistic infections • longer life expectancy • less transmission.
Before choosing ART
ART history/naivety • comorbidities • baseline viral load • drug-drug interactions • HIV resistance • adherence/convenience • pregnancy/breastfeeding.

What / When to Start

Deck: start ART as soon as possible regardless of CD4 → rapid entry • Test & Treat • treatment as prevention.

PregnancyDeck names bictegravir/dolutegravir as preferred INSTIs; darunavir/ritonavir as preferred PI
HBV coinfectionGenerally include 2 NRTIs with anti-HBV activity; flare possible if stopped/switched
Rapid-start exceptions on deck: cryptococcal meningitis • tuberculosis meningitis.

IRIS

Unmasking
Previously occult infection becomes apparent after immune recovery.
Paradoxical
Known/treated infection appears to worsen after immune recovery.

Prevention

ConceptUse
U=U / TasPSuppressed viral load prevents sexual transmission
PrEPBefore exposure; deck says daily or intermittent; efficacy rises with adherence
PEPAfter exposure; occupational exposure / sexual assault etc.
Handout practical PEP: 3-drug ART for 28 days; start ASAP and no later than 72 h after significant exposure.
