Oncogenic Viruses - Factoid Sheet

Week 8 • Core Lecture 5 • current Thai deck controls
Master map: oncogenic viruses either press the gas (growth signaling/oncogene activation) or cut the brakes (p53/RB/apoptosis control).

General Principles

Handout-only board link: EBV uses CD21 on B cells; endemic Burkitt may show jaw mass + starry-sky histology.

Vignette Shortcuts

  • African jaw tumor + MYC 8;14 → EBV/Burkitt
  • Koilocyte / cervical dysplasia → HPV
  • Cauliflower genital wart → HPV 6/11
  • Violaceous lesion + spindle cells/slit spaces → HHV-8/Kaposi
  • Chronic viral hepatitis + HCC → chronic injury/regeneration; HBV can also integrate
Final anchors: EBV = LMP-1/EBNA-2 + MYC • HPV = E6/p53 + E7/RB + koilocyte • HHV-8 = anti-apoptosis/growth signals + Kaposi spindle vessels.
