Technique trap: under-decolorize → organisms can look falsely Gram+. Over-decolorize → organisms can look falsely Gram-.

Division + Spores + Exam Traps

Bacterial Division

FtsZ = Z ring. Chromosome replicates/separates → Fts/divisome localizes → Z ring constricts → peptidoglycan/septum synthesis → daughter cells.

Endospore Formation

StepSequence in deck
1DNA replication + axial filament
2Asymmetric septum separates forespore / mother cell
3Mother cell engulfs forespore → second membrane
4Peptidoglycan layer develops between membranes
5Calcium added to core; spore matures
6Mother cell releases mature spore
Spore survival: extreme heat • lack of water • toxic chemicals • radiation.
