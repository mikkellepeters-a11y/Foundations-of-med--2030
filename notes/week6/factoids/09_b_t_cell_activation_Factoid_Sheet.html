B Cell and T Cell Activation

Co-stimulation, helper subsets, B-cell responses, memory, ILCs, and JAK-STAT signaling

Core 09High-density reviewSource-derived

B-cell activation map

  • BCR captures antigen -> B cell presents peptide on MHC II -> helper T-cell recognition.
  • CD40L/CD40 plus cytokines drive the T-dependent response.
  • Outputs include plasma cells, germinal-center responses, class switching, affinity maturation, and memory.

T-independent response

  • Strong repetitive antigen can activate B cells without helper-T-cell support.
  • The response is predominantly IgM with weak class switching and poor durable memory.
  • Conjugate vaccines attach protein to polysaccharide so a polysaccharide-specific B cell can recruit T-cell help.

T-cell activation

  • Peptide-MHC recognition alone is not enough for naive T-cell activation.
  • B7-CD28 costimulation is a key second signal and protects against inappropriate activation.
  • Helper T-cell differentiation includes Th1, Th2, Th17, and Treg patterns.

Other high-yield links

  • Innate lymphoid cells reproduce helper-like cytokine patterns without antigen-specific receptors.
  • Cytokine signaling is framed as receptor assembly -> JAK -> STAT -> nucleus.
  • Pathway defects and drugs targeting these signaling steps are clinically testable.
