Basics of Innate Immunity I

Innate cells, pattern-recognition receptors, cytokines, complement, and complement regulation

Core 02High-density reviewSource-derived

If you remember nothing else

  • Know the major cells that participate in innate immune responses and the roles they play.
  • Pattern-recognition receptors detect recurring microbial or danger-associated patterns.
  • Pro-inflammatory and anti-inflammatory cytokines coordinate the intensity and direction of innate responses.
  • Complement can attack pathogens directly, opsonize them, amplify inflammation, and interact with immune cells.

Complement map

  • Classical, lectin, and alternative pathways converge on shared downstream complement functions.
  • Complement is powerful enough that regulation is essential; loss of regulation can produce disease.
  • The lecture specifically connects complement-regulatory disorders with paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria and angioedema.

PRR families emphasized

  • Toll-like receptors (TLRs).
  • NOD-like receptors (NLRs).
  • RIG-like receptors (RLRs), STING, C-type lectin receptors, scavenger receptors, and complement receptors.

Exam frame

  • Separate the initiating pathway from the shared complement effector functions.
  • When a vignette is about uncontrolled complement activity, think about a failure of regulation rather than simply 'too much immunity.'
  • Keep cell type, receptor type, cytokine signal, and complement function as four separate layers.
