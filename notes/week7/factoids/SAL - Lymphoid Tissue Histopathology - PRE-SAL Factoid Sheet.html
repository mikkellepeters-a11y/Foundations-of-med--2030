Lymph node zones

CortexB-cell follicles; secondary follicle = pale germinal center + darker mantle
ParacortexT cells + HEVs; lymphocytes enter from blood here
Medullary cordsPlasma cells, lymphocytes, macrophages
Medullary sinusesMacrophage-rich lymph channels
HilumArtery, vein, efferent lymphatic

Primary follicle

Dark, fairly uniform resting B-cell nodule; no pale germinal center.

Secondary follicle

Pale germinal center + mantle zone; active B-cell proliferation, affinity maturation, class switching.

Traffic cross-link

Antigen/DCs: afferent lymphatic -> subcapsular sinus -> cortex/paracortex.
Naive B/T cells: blood -> HEV in paracortex.
Exit: efferent lymphatic at hilum.
B/T map: node cortex B / paracortex T • spleen follicle B / PALS T • thymus = T-lineage organ, no B-cell follicles.
