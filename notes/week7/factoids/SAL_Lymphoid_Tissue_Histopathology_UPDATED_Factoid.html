Diffuse tissue + nodules

StructureReleased-slide recognitionKey phrase
Diffuse lymphatic tissueScattered lymphocytes with dark nuclei and scant cytoplasmCan be solitary or part of tonsil/Peyer's
Primary noduleMore organized lymphocytes supported by reticular connective tissueReticular fibers = Type III collagen network
Secondary noduleLike primary but with a pale germinal centerGerminal center contains maturing lymphocytes
Peyer's patchLymphoid tissue in the mucosa of the ileumNodules + diffuse lymphoid tissue
Primary vs secondary nodule: if you see a pale germinal center, call it secondary.
