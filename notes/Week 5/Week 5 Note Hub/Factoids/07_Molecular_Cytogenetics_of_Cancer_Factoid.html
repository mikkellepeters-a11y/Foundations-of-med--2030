Molecular Cytogenetics of Cancer

Master framework: Cancer cytogenetics links genomic instability, translocations, and gene amplification to prognosis, therapy, and measurable residual disease.

Rearrangements

ConceptHigh-yield fact
t(14;18)BCL2 overexpression; follicular lymphoma; apoptosis resistance.
t(9;22)BCR-ABL; constitutive tyrosine kinase; CML and some ALL.
Detection sensitivityFor BCR-ABL monitoring: karyotype < FISH < quantitative RT-PCR.

Amplification

ConceptHigh-yield fact
MDM2Suppresses p53; important in selected sarcomas.
MYCNNeuroblastoma amplification = adverse prognosis.
CCND1/Cyclin D1Promotes G1→S; pathway targetable with CDK4/6 inhibition in selected breast cancer.
HER2Aggressive biology but targetable receptor amplification.
Double minutes / HSRExtrachromosomal / intrachromosomal patterns of amplification.

Genome protection

ConceptHigh-yield fact
p53Guardian of the genome: arrest, repair, senescence, or apoptosis after damage.
Genomic instabilityPromotes aneuploidy, deletions, translocations, and amplification.

Exam traps

  • Gene amplification means increased copy number, not merely increased transcription.
  • HER2 can be both adverse biology and a therapeutic vulnerability.
  • BCL2 overexpression can blunt chemotherapy-induced apoptosis.
Last 30 seconds: t14;18=BCL2 • t9;22=BCR-ABL • p53 guardian • MDM2/MYCN/CCND1/HER2 amplification • qRT-PCR most sensitive for BCR-ABL monitoring.
