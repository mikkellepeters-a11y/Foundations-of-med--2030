Carcinogenesis and Epidemiology

Master framework: Cancer emerges when growth activation, tumor-suppressor loss, apoptosis escape, and repair failure accumulate under mutagenic selection.

Gene classes

ConceptHigh-yield fact
OncogeneActivated proto-oncogene; gain-of-function; one activated allele may be sufficient at the cellular level.
Tumor suppressorLoss/inactivation of a growth brake; often requires loss of both functional copies in a tumor cell.
Apoptosis regulatorChanges survival/death balance; BCL2 overexpression promotes survival.
DNA-repair geneRepair failure increases mutation rate and genomic instability.

Carcinogens

ConceptHigh-yield fact
UVBPyrimidine dimers; nucleotide-excision repair connection.
Ionizing radiationDNA strand breaks.
HPVE6 inhibits p53; E7 inhibits RB.
Chemical carcinogensMany require metabolic activation and form DNA adducts/mutations.

Epidemiology

ConceptHigh-yield fact
IncidenceNew cases in a population over time.
MortalityDeaths due to the cancer over time.

Exam traps

  • Oncogene = accelerator stuck ON; tumor suppressor = brake lost.
  • p53 responds to damage; it is not itself a DNA-repair enzyme.
  • Incidence and mortality rankings answer different questions.
Last 30 seconds: Oncogene GOF • TSG LOF • BCL2 blocks apoptosis • Repair failure → instability • UVB/NER • HPV E6=p53, E7=RB.
