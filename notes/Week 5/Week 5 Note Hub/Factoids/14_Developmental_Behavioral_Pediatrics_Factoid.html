Foundations of Medicine · Week 5 Factoid Sheet

Introduction to Developmental-Behavioral Pediatrics

Master framework: Development is patterned but variable and plastic; outcomes emerge from biology interacting with relationships, environment, culture, adversity, and resilience.

Development framework

ConceptHigh-yield fact
SequencePredictable broad sequence with individual variability.
CephalocaudalDevelopment progresses head → lower body.
ProximodistalControl develops center → distal extremities.
DomainsGross motor, fine motor, speech/language, cognitive, social-emotional, adaptive.

Risk & resilience

ConceptHigh-yield fact
Nature + nurtureGenes, epigenetics, prenatal factors, illness, family, culture, education, community, supports interact.
Toxic stress / ACEsIncrease population-level lifelong risk; do not determine an individual child's destiny.
ResilienceSupportive adults, self-regulation/self-efficacy, hope/faith/culture, positive childhood experiences.

Clinical relationship

ConceptHigh-yield fact
Therapeutic allianceShared goals + shared tasks + relational bond.
SHARESet/support • Hear • Address/Allow • Reflect/Reframe/Revisit • Empower.
Implicit biasCan change how disability, behavior, and opportunities are framed.
Social model of disabilityDisability can arise from person-environment mismatch; remove barriers and include the patient directly.
HistoryUse culturally and linguistically sensitive, patient- and family-centered communication.

Exam traps

  • ACE score is a risk marker, not destiny.
  • Family-centered care does not mean speaking only to the caregiver.
  • Developmental variability is expected, but persistent concerning patterns still warrant assessment.
Last 30 seconds: 6 domains • cephalocaudal/proximodistal • ACEs raise risk, not fate • resilience matters • alliance = goals/tasks/bond • SHARE • remove barriers.
