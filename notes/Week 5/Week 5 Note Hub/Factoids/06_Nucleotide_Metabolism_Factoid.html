Nucleotide Metabolism

Master framework: Tie purine/pyrimidine synthesis to salvage disorders, orotic acid patterns, tumor lysis, and antimetabolite mechanisms.

Purines

ConceptHigh-yield fact
De novo purine synthesisBuilds the purine ring on PRPP.
HGPRTSalvages hypoxanthine and guanine.
Lesch-NyhanHGPRT deficiency → excess uric acid + neurologic dysfunction/self-injury.
ADA deficiencyToxic deoxyadenosine metabolites injure lymphocytes → SCID.
Tumor lysis↑ uric acid, K, phosphate; ↓ Ca.

Pyrimidines & drugs

ConceptHigh-yield fact
UMP synthase deficiencyOrotic aciduria + megaloblastic anemia; no hyperammonemia.
OTC deficiencyOrotic acid + hyperammonemia.
Ribonucleotide reductaseMakes deoxyribonucleotides; inhibited by hydroxyurea.
5-FUInhibits thymidylate synthase.
MethotrexateInhibits DHFR.
6-MPInhibits de novo purine synthesis; inactivated by xanthine oxidase.

Exam traps

  • Orotic acid + hyperammonemia = think OTC, not UMP synthase.
  • Allopurinol/febuxostat can raise 6-MP exposure by reducing xanthine-oxidase metabolism.
  • Tumor lysis connects nucleic-acid breakdown to uric acid.
Last 30 seconds: HGPRT = salvage • ADA → SCID • UMP synthase = orotic/no NH3 • OTC = orotic + NH3 • 5-FU TS • MTX DHFR • 6-MP purines.
