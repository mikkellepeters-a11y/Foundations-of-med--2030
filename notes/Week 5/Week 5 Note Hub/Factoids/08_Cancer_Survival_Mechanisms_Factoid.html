Foundations of Medicine · Week 5 Factoid Sheet

Cancer Survival Mechanisms

Master framework: Survival comes from checkpoint escape, apoptosis resistance, telomere maintenance, metabolic reprogramming, angiogenesis, invasion, and clonal heterogeneity.

Cell cycle & death

ConceptHigh-yield fact
p53 → p21CDK inhibition keeps RB active and restrains E2F → G1/S arrest.
BCL2/BCL-XLAnti-apoptotic.
BAX/PUMAPro-apoptotic.
VenetoclaxTargets BCL2 in selected hematologic malignancies.

Immortality & metabolism

ConceptHigh-yield fact
TelomeraseMaintains TTAGGG telomeres and delays senescence.
Warburg effectAerobic glycolysis with high glucose uptake/lactate; supports biosynthesis; FDG-PET avidity.
Mutant IDH1/2Produces 2-hydroxyglutarate → altered methylation and blocked differentiation.

Angiogenesis & metastasis

ConceptHigh-yield fact
HypoxiaStabilizes HIF-1α → VEGF → angiogenesis.
Metastatic sequenceLoss of adhesion/ECM invasion → intravasation → circulation survival → extravasation → colonization.
ResistanceHeterogeneity and stem-like populations allow resistant clones to repopulate.

Exam traps

  • RB restrains E2F when hypophosphorylated/active.
  • FDG-PET exploits high glucose uptake, not direct telomerase activity.
  • Therapy itself creates selection pressure favoring resistant subclones.
Last 30 seconds: p53→p21→RB/E2F arrest • BCL2 anti-apoptosis • Telomerase • Warburg/FDG • IDH→2-HG • HIF→VEGF • heterogeneity→resistance.
