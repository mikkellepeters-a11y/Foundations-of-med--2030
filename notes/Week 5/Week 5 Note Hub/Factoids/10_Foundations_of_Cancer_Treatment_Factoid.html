Foundations of Cancer Treatment

Master framework: Know local versus systemic modalities, radiation biology, log-kill/pulsed chemotherapy, classic toxicities, and urgent oncologic complications.

Modalities

ConceptHigh-yield fact
SurgeryPrimarily local; diagnostic, curative, cytoreductive, prophylactic, palliative, reconstructive roles.
RadiationLocal/regional; direct DNA damage + ROS-mediated damage.
ChemotherapySystemic; especially central in hematologic/metastatic disease.

Radiation/chemo principles

ConceptHigh-yield fact
Radiosensitive phaseLate G2/M.
Relatively radioresistantLate S.
FractionationPermits normal-tissue repair, redistribution, reoxygenation, repopulation effects.
Log-killEach cycle kills a constant fraction, not a constant number.
Pulsed therapyTreatment cycles separated by recovery periods.

Toxicity anchors

ConceptHigh-yield fact
CisplatinNephrotoxicity + ototoxicity.
VincristinePeripheral neuropathy.
BleomycinPulmonary toxicity/fibrosis.
DoxorubicinCardiotoxicity.
CyclophosphamideHemorrhagic cystitis.
Neutropenic feverUrgent broad antipseudomonal coverage.
Tumor lysis↑ K/phosphate/uric acid, ↓ Ca.

Exam traps

  • Surgery/radiation are mainly local; chemotherapy/targeted/immunotherapy are systemic.
  • Antimetabolites are S-phase specific; vincas/taxanes are M-phase specific.
  • Febrile neutropenia is treated urgently rather than awaiting cultures.
Last 30 seconds: Local: surgery/radiation • Systemic: chemo • G2/M radiosensitive • Log-kill=fraction • Cisplatin kidney/ear • Doxo heart • Cyclophosphamide bladder.
