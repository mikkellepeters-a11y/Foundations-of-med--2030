Molecular Diagnostics

Master framework: Choose the assay based on what must be measured: DNA, RNA, protein, sequence, methylation, or polymorphic identity markers.

Core methods

ConceptHigh-yield fact
PCRAmplifies a defined DNA sequence.
RT-PCRRNA → cDNA → amplification; useful for RNA targets/gene expression.
cDNAMade from mature mRNA, so introns are absent.
Southern blotDNA.
Northern blotRNA.
Western blotProtein.
Sanger sequencingddNTP chain termination; ddNTP lacks a 3′-OH.
Bisulfite sequencingAssesses DNA methylation patterns.
STR/VNTR/RFLPIdentity, paternity, forensics, linkage/variant analysis.

Scenario matching

ConceptHigh-yield fact
Detect a known DNA targetPCR.
Quantify an RNA transcriptRT-PCR after reverse transcription.
Express a human coding sequence in bacteriaUse intron-free cDNA.
Identify a proteinWestern blot.
Targeted methylation questionBisulfite-based analysis.

Exam traps

  • SNoW DRoP: Southern DNA, Northern RNA, Western Protein.
  • PCR does not begin with RNA unless reverse transcription is performed first.
  • Sanger termination occurs because ddNTP lacks the 3′-OH needed for extension.
Last 30 seconds: PCR = DNA • RT-PCR = RNA→cDNA • Southern/Northern/Western = DNA/RNA/protein • Sanger = ddNTP • cDNA lacks introns.
