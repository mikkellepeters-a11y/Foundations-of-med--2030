Part II: Answer Key & Explanations

1. C — Base management on personal and family history rather than treating the VUS as pathogenicStep 1-styleLecture 12

Reasoning pathway: A VUS should not drive irreversible management.

Important distractor distinction: Its significance is unknown, not positive or negative.

2. A — High glucose uptake associated with aerobic glycolysisIn-houseLecture 10

Reasoning pathway: Many cancers preferentially use aerobic glycolysis and avidly take up FDG.

Important distractor distinction: PET exploits metabolism rather than apoptosis.

3. C — ReoxygenationIntermediateLecture 9

Reasoning pathway: Tumor shrinkage can improve oxygenation of formerly hypoxic cells.

Important distractor distinction: Oxygen enhances radiation effectiveness.

4. E — APC-mediated Wnt regulationIntermediateLecture 2

Reasoning pathway: FAP is caused by APC loss and is typically microsatellite stable.

Important distractor distinction: Lynch syndrome involves MMR and usually lacks massive polyposis.

5. C — The patient's own T cells are engineered to recognize a chosen tumor antigenIntermediateLecture 11

Reasoning pathway: CAR-T uses autologous T cells modified ex vivo for tumor recognition.

Important distractor distinction: It is a cellular precision therapy.

6. A — They may benefit from cascade testing for a germline mismatch-repair variantStep 1-styleLecture 2

Reasoning pathway: The tumor pattern suggests Lynch syndrome, so a confirmed germline variant has implications for relatives.

Important distractor distinction: MEN2 and BRCA-associated strategies address different pathways.

7. E — Xanthine oxidase inhibition reduces azathioprine/6-MP inactivationStep 1-styleLecture 6

Reasoning pathway: 6-MP and azathioprine are inactivated by xanthine oxidase.

Important distractor distinction: The dose should be reduced when xanthine oxidase is inhibited.

8. D — Each dose kills a fraction rather than every remaining cellIntermediateLecture 9

Reasoning pathway: The log cell-kill model predicts residual tumor after each cycle.

Important distractor distinction: Repeated treatment progressively reduces the remaining burden.

9. A — The T cell receives an inhibitory signal and becomes less effective against the tumorStep 1-styleLecture 10

Reasoning pathway: PD-1/PD-L1 is an immune checkpoint exploited by tumors.

Important distractor distinction: Blocking the interaction can restore antitumor T-cell activity.

10. D — Intensive MRI-based surveillance, including whole-body imagingStep 1-styleLecture 2

Reasoning pathway: Li-Fraumeni syndrome requires intensive lifelong surveillance and minimizes radiation when feasible.

Important distractor distinction: The syndrome carries broad early-onset cancer risk.

11. B — Extent of spread, or stageIntermediateLecture 1

Reasoning pathway: Stage is the most important prognostic factor for most cancers.

Important distractor distinction: Grade matters, but generally less than stage.

12. A — Reverse-transcriptase PCR using cDNA generated from the mRNA IntermediateLecture 3: Molecular Diagnostics

Reasoning pathway: RT-PCR first converts RNA to cDNA and then amplifies the target with PCR, allowing assessment of gene expression.

Important distractor distinction: Standard DNA assays do not directly amplify an RNA template.

13. C — Uridine or UMP supplementationIntermediateLecture 6

Reasoning pathway: UMP synthase deficiency can be bypassed with uridine/UMP to restore pyrimidine availability.

Important distractor distinction: Normal ammonia helps distinguish it from OTC deficiency.

14. D — MorphineStep 1-styleLecture 13

Reasoning pathway: Low-dose opioids are the gold standard for symptomatic dyspnea.

Important distractor distinction: Oxygen is most useful when hypoxemia is present.

15. A — SensitivityIn-houseLecture 8

Reasoning pathway: Higher sensitivity means fewer diseased patients are missed.

Important distractor distinction: Specificity addresses false positives.

16. C — ImatinibIntermediateLecture 7

Reasoning pathway: Imatinib targets BCR-ABL in CML.

Important distractor distinction: The other drugs target hormones, VEGF, folate rescue, or uric acid metabolism.

17. D — PRPPIn-houseLecture 6

Reasoning pathway: HGPRT deficiency leaves excess PRPP and drives purine overproduction.

Important distractor distinction: The other metabolites belong to pyrimidine or folate pathways.

18. B — Balanced Robertsonian translocation IntermediateLecture 4: Cytogenetics

Reasoning pathway: A balanced Robertsonian carrier may have 45 chromosomes because two acrocentric long arms are fused while the carrier remains phenotypically normal.

Important distractor distinction: The reproductive risk comes from formation of unbalanced gametes, not from the carrier necessarily being abnormal.

19. E — At any stage of a serious illness, including while curative treatment continuesIn-houseLecture 13

Reasoning pathway: Palliative care can be concurrent with disease-directed therapy.

Important distractor distinction: Hospice is the more end-of-life-specific service.

20. C — A distant metastatic deposit from the tumorIntermediateLecture 1

Reasoning pathway: Metastasis unequivocally establishes malignancy.

Important distractor distinction: Atypia is suggestive, but metastasis is definitive.

21. D — Lead-time biasStep 1-styleLecture 8

Reasoning pathway: Earlier diagnosis can inflate survival time without changing the date of death.

Important distractor distinction: Mortality rather than survival-from-diagnosis helps avoid this illusion.

22. A — Checkpoint blockade released inhibitory control over T cellsIntermediateLecture 9

Reasoning pathway: Immune checkpoint inhibitors can cause immune-related adverse events by generalized T-cell activation.

Important distractor distinction: This toxicity differs from classic cytotoxic chemotherapy.

23. B — p53 and RbStep 1-styleLecture 5

Reasoning pathway: E6 inhibits p53 and E7 inhibits Rb.

Important distractor distinction: The pairing directly removes two key cell-cycle/apoptosis safeguards.

24. E — Stent thrombosis from inadequate prodrug activationIntermediateLecture 11

Reasoning pathway: Clopidogrel requires CYP2C19 activation.

Important distractor distinction: Poor activation reduces antiplatelet effect.

25. B — Confirm amplification with FISHStep 1-styleLecture 7

Reasoning pathway: Equivocal HER2 IHC requires confirmatory FISH.

Important distractor distinction: A 2+ result is neither clearly positive nor negative.

26. D — Northern blot In-houseLecture 3: Molecular Diagnostics

Reasoning pathway: Northern blotting detects RNA and is commonly used to assess gene expression.

Important distractor distinction: Southern detects DNA and Western detects protein.

27. E — t(14;18)In-houseLecture 7

Reasoning pathway: t(14;18) drives BCL2 overexpression in follicular lymphoma.

Important distractor distinction: The other translocations involve different drivers.

28. B — Slow-growing tumors remain detectable for longer and are more likely to be capturedIntermediateLecture 8

Reasoning pathway: Length-time bias enriches screened cohorts for indolent disease.

Important distractor distinction: Aggressive tumors may arise between screening intervals.

29. C — Reduced DPD activityStep 1-styleLecture 11

Reasoning pathway: DPD breaks down 5-FU; deficiency causes excessive exposure.

Important distractor distinction: UGT1A1 is more relevant to irinotecan.

30. A — Ribonucleotide reductaseStep 1-styleLecture 6

Reasoning pathway: High dATP inhibits ribonucleotide reductase, limiting deoxyribonucleotide production.

Important distractor distinction: This is the biochemical basis of lymphocyte toxicity in ADA deficiency.

31. B — NeoplasiaIn-houseLecture 1

Reasoning pathway: Neoplasia is unregulated, irreversible, and monoclonal, unlike stimulus-dependent polyclonal hyperplasia.

Important distractor distinction: Reactive and physiologic hyperplasia should regress when the stimulus is removed.

32. C — Life insurance underwritingIntermediateLecture 12

Reasoning pathway: GINA protects health insurance and employment, but not life, disability, or long-term-care insurance in the same way.

Important distractor distinction: Its protections are important but limited.

33. E — Dependence on remaining DNA-repair pathways that can be exploited with PARP inhibitionStep 1-styleLecture 2

Reasoning pathway: BRCA loss impairs double-strand repair and can create sensitivity to PARP inhibition.

Important distractor distinction: The other targets belong to unrelated molecular drivers.

34. D — Immediate empiric antibioticsStep 1-styleLecture 9

Reasoning pathway: Neutropenic fever is an oncologic emergency.

Important distractor distinction: Treatment should not be delayed while cultures are pending.

35. A — Low-dose CTIntermediateLecture 8

Reasoning pathway: Annual low-dose CT is recommended for appropriate high-risk current/former smokers.

Important distractor distinction: Chest x-ray is not the preferred screening modality.

36. B — Dihydrofolate reductaseIntermediateLecture 6

Reasoning pathway: Methotrexate inhibits DHFR and lowers THF availability.

Important distractor distinction: 5-FU targets thymidylate synthase instead.

37. E — Rb remains hypophosphorylated and continues binding E2FStep 1-styleLecture 10

Reasoning pathway: CDK4/6 blockade prevents Rb phosphorylation and maintains the G1 brake.

Important distractor distinction: Loss of RB1 can cause resistance.

38. D — Metaphase In-houseLecture 4: Cytogenetics

Reasoning pathway: Cells are arrested in metaphase, when chromosomes are condensed and individually visible.

Important distractor distinction: Interphase chromosomes are decondensed chromatin and are not ideal for conventional karyotype analysis.

39. E — Refer for formal genetic counseling and risk assessmentIntermediateLecture 12

Reasoning pathway: Multiple hereditary-cancer red flags warrant counseling and consideration of testing.

Important distractor distinction: Counseling should precede major management decisions.

40. B — Local invasion through extracellular matrix and basement membraneStep 1-styleLecture 10

Reasoning pathway: Loss of adhesion plus matrix degradation enables invasion.

Important distractor distinction: These are core components of the metastatic cascade.
