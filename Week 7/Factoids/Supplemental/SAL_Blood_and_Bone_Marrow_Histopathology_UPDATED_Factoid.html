Lymphocyte • monocyte. Lymphocyte = spherical/scant cytoplasm; monocyte = large/horseshoe nucleus.

Platelets - Slide 7

Small fragmented elements in peripheral blood. They are fragments of megakaryocytes found in bone marrow and have important functions in blood clotting and wound healing.

Peripheral blood - Slide 8

Whole-field gestalt: erythrocytes dominate the field; leukocytes are scattered; platelets are tiny. Learn the overall “open” look before zooming in on any one cell.

Self-check from the released images

  • Can you find a leukocyte before looking for its label?
  • Can you identify the tiny platelet fragments without confusing them with stain debris?
  • Can you tell lymphocyte from monocyte using nucleus shape and amount of cytoplasm?
