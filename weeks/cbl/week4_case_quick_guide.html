Why Females Can Look Different

Mosaicism: females have two X chromosomes, and one X is randomly inactivated in each cell. In Fragile X, some cells can therefore still express FMR1 gene product, whereas an affected male with a silenced FMR1 allele does not have a second X copy to provide expression.

Anticipation

Anticipation = symptoms become more severe from one generation to the next.
Fragile X mechanism in source: CGG trinucleotide repeats accumulate in the 5′ untranslated region. Increasing repeat burden can promote methylation and silencing of FMR1.

Repeat-Number Map

CGG repeatsCategory / effect in source
5–44Common / normal; no effect
45–54Gray-zone / intermediate allele; no effect
55–200Premutation; source notes risk of fragile-X-associated primary ovarian insufficiency in women and fragile-X-associated tremor/ataxia syndrome in men or women
>200Full mutation; Fragile X syndrome, more severe in men than women
Expression pearl: only with a high number of repeats does methylation of FMR1 become stimulated, reducing or eliminating gene expression and therefore FMRP production.

Phenotype + Counseling + Screening

Physical / Reproductive Features

Facial features
Long, narrow face • prominent forehead • large ears • prominent chin. These are described as more pronounced in males.
Males
Macroorchidism may become noticeable after puberty.
Females: menstrual irregularities • premature ovarian failure / early menopause • fertility issues.

Disclosing a Diagnosis

Four ethical principles: Justice • Non-maleficence • Autonomy • Beneficence.

Why Fragile X Is Not Routine Newborn Screening

Screening criterionWhat the source says
IncidenceThe disorder should have a high incidence.
TreatmentThere should be an effective yet not overly expensive treatment.
Test costThe screening test should be inexpensive.
PerformanceThe screening should have high sensitivity and specificity.
Fragile X: the source says the screening technique is straightforward and has high sensitivity/specificity, but there is no effective cure; therefore it is not routinely included in newborn screening.
