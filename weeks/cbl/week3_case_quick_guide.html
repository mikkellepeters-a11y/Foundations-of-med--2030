Week 3 CBL Case - Quick Guide

Foundations of Medicine • CBL • supplied high-yield document only
Master chain: HbS mutation → deoxygenation-driven polymerization → rigid sickled RBCs → endothelial adhesion + aggregates → microvascular occlusion → local hypoxia → inflammation / reperfusion injury → pain + organ damage.

Screening + Genetics

Newborn screen
Blood test on the 1st or 2nd day of life; results forwarded to the PCP by about the second week. Delayed diagnosis may occur when a patient was born where universal newborn screening was unavailable.
Trait vs disease
One defective copy → sickle cell trait / carrier. Two defective copies → sickle cell disease.
Family example in source: father with SCD + mother with trait → ~50% trait and ~50% SCD among offspring.

Why Sickle Cell Pain Happens

Core physical problem
Triggers promote Hb polymerization → RBCs become rigid. Adhesion molecules increase interaction between sickled RBCs and endothelium; heterocellular aggregates obstruct small vessels.
Amplifiers
Local hypoxia feeds more sickling. The source also lists inflammatory mediators/free radicals, neutrophil adhesiveness, platelet activation, and hypercoagulability.
VOC triggers: hypoxia • cold weather • infection • acidosis • dehydration • alcohol • stress • pregnancy.

HbS Mutation + Electrophoresis

FeatureHigh-yield fact
Mutationβ-globin codon 6: GAG → GTG; glutamic acid → valine.
Mutation typeQualitative missense mutation.
ChemistryValine is hydrophobic; glutamic acid is hydrophilic and negatively charged at alkaline pH.
ElectrophoresisNormal Hb has extra negative charge and migrates faster toward the positive pole; HbS moves more slowly.
DeoxygenationHbS tetramers aggregate into arrays → RBC deformation, rigidity, and poor capillary transit.

Baseline Hemolysis Pattern

Expected
↓ Hgb/Hct • ↑ RDW • ↑ reticulocytes (appropriate marrow response) • hyperbilirubinemia.
Source lab pearls
CK may appear elevated because commercial tests can capture adenylate kinase from RBC lysis. Potassium may be high on the first CMP from RBC lysis and normalize with IV fluids.

Acute chest syndrome: pulmonary infiltrate + abnormal lung findings should raise suspicion. The source states treatment includes emergent red blood cell exchange.

Chronic / Vaso-occlusive Sequelae

Bone / vascular
Femoral or humeral head osteonecrosis from vaso-occlusion plus increased marrow pressure.
Other
Priapism • proliferative retinopathy • renal complications.

Hydroxyurea / HbF

Electrophoresis after treatment
The supplied document says hydroxyurea stimulates re-expression of fetal hemoglobin.
HbF composition
α₂γ₂, versus normal adult Hb α₂β₂; different amino-acid composition → different migration speed.

Probability Pearl from the Case

Source example: if 1/200 women in the stated population carry sickle trait and Mr. Owens has SCD, the document calculates a child’s SCD risk as 1/400: woman passes HbS with 1/200 × 1/2 = 1/400, while Mr. Owens passes HbS with probability 1.
Last 30 seconds: GAG→GTG / Glu→Val • HbS less negative → slower electrophoresis • VOC = polymerization + adhesion + occlusion + hypoxia • sequestration = big spleen + acute Hgb drop • aplastic = parvo + retic ↓ • ACS = infiltrate + hypoxia → exchange • hydroxyurea → HbF α₂γ₂.
