Epithelial Tissue Histology - Factoid Sheet

Week 3 • SAL • current deck primary
Core identity: epithelial tissue = highly cellular + avascular + polarized + junction-rich + attached to basal lamina.

Pemphigus: loss of desmosomal adhesion → epithelial detachment/blisters.
Smoking metaplasia: airway epithelium can shift toward stratified squamous, sacrificing normal specialized function.
EMT: epithelial cells lose adhesion/polarity and gain motility → important in carcinoma invasion/metastasis.
Last 30 sec: simple=one layer • pseudo=all cells touch basement membrane • cilia=microtubules • microvilli/stereocilia=actin • tight seals • adherens=actin • desmosome=IF cell-cell • hemidesmosome=IF cell-matrix • gap communicates • merocrine/apocrine/holocrine.
