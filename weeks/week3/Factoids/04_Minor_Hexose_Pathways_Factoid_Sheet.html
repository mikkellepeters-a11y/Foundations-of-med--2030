Polyol Pathway

Glucose --aldose reductase + NADPH→ sorbitol --sorbitol dehydrogenase→ fructose.
Galactose --aldose reductase→ galactitol; humans cannot metabolize galactitol effectively → osmotic cataracts.
Diabetes: high glucose drives aldose reductase; sorbitol accumulation is emphasized in vulnerable tissues with low sorbitol-processing capacity.
