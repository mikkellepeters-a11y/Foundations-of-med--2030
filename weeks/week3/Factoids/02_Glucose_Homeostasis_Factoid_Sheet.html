Fed vs Fasting

Fed
High insulin/glucagon ratio → liver stores glucose → glycogenesis favored.
Fasting
High glucagon/insulin ratio → liver releases glucose → glycogenolysis + gluconeogenesis favored.
Liver has glucose-6-phosphatase. Muscle does not. Liver glycogen can support blood glucose; muscle glycogen is for muscle.
