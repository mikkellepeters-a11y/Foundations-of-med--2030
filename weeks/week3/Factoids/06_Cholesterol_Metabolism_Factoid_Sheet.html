Cholesterol Synthesis

ConceptHigh yield
Main siteMostly liver; endogenous synthesis exceeds typical dietary input in the lecture.
Rate-limiting enzymeHMG-CoA reductase: HMG-CoA → mevalonate.
InsulinFavors phosphatase activity → dephosphorylated/active reductase.
GlucagonFavors kinase activity → phosphorylated/inactive reductase.
High cholesterol / bile saltsNegative feedback; suppress activity/expression.
StatinsPharmacologic HMG-CoA reductase inhibition.

SREBP Long-Term Regulation

Low cholesterol
SREBP-SCAP traffics to Golgi → S1P/S2P cleavage → nuclear SREBP → ↑ HMG-CoA reductase transcription.
High cholesterol
SCAP/INSIG retains the complex in ER → ↓ transcription.

Bile Acids

StepAnchor
Rate-limitingCYP7A1 / cholesterol 7α-hydroxylase

ConjugationGlycine / taurine → bile salts

RecyclingMost bile acids undergo enterohepatic recirculation.
Net eliminationSmall fecal loss = major route for cholesterol disposal.

Cholelithiasis

Cholesterol : bile salts ratio rises → bile becomes supersaturated → cholesterol precipitates → stones.
Deck-linked causes of bile-salt deficiency: biliary obstruction • intestinal malabsorption • decreased synthesis.
Last 30 seconds: HMGCR • insulin ON / glucagon OFF • statins • SREBP low-cholesterol response • CYP7A1 • bile-acid recycling • fecal loss • supersaturated bile = cholesterol stones.
