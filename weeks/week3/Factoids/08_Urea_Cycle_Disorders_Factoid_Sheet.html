Urea Cycle Disorders - Factoid Sheet

Week 3 • Wednesday Lecture 8 • current deck primary
Master chain: transamination collects nitrogen on glutamate → glutamine/alanine carry it safely → liver releases NH₃ → urea cycle converts NH₃ to urea → kidney excretes urea.

Nitrogen Handling

EnzymeRole
AminotransferasesAA + α-KG ⇌ α-ketoacid + glutamate; require PLP/B6.
Glutamate dehydrogenaseGlutamate → α-KG + NH₃; oxidative deamination, especially liver.
Glutamine synthetaseCaptures NH₃ as glutamine in peripheral tissues/brain.
GlutaminaseReleases NH₃ from glutamine in liver and kidney; renal activity rises in acidosis.

Urea Cycle

StepAnchor
1CPS-I: NH₃ + CO₂ → carbamoyl phosphate; mitochondria; needs NAG.
2OTC: carbamoyl phosphate + ornithine → citrulline; mitochondria.
3Citrulline + aspartate → argininosuccinate.
4Argininosuccinate → arginine + fumarate.
5Arginine → urea + ornithine.

Liver failure
↓ urea synthesis → low BUN + high ammonia.
Kidney failure
Urea made but not excreted → high BUN.
Treatment logic: reduce protein nitrogen + maintain calories + use nitrogen scavengers (benzoate; phenylacetate/phenylbutyrate).
