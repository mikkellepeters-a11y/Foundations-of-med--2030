Coping with Chronic Pain - Factoid Sheet

Week 3 • Holistic Patient Care • uploaded 2026 lecture + included handout only
Master idea: nociception is sensory detection; pain is the brain-generated experience after biological, psychological, and social inputs are integrated.

Three Mechanistic Pain Types

TypeProblemClue
NociceptiveTissue damage/threatExam and pain match the injured tissue
NeuropathicNerve/spinal cord lesion or diseaseSensory loss/allodynia in nerve territory; abnormal EMG may support
NociplasticPain system amplifies despite no sufficient tissue or nerve lesion≥3 mo regional/widespread pain + hypersensitivity + fatigue/sleep/cognitive/sensory features + no better explanation
Nociplastic ≠ “normal scan.” It is a positive clinical pattern, not simply a diagnosis of exclusion.

Coping + Treatment

PatternMeaning
Catastrophizing (PCS)Rumination + magnification + helplessness
Fear of movement (TSK)Pain interpreted as damage → avoidance
Self-efficacy (PSEQ)Confidence to function despite pain
Acceptance (CPAQ)Willingness + continued meaningful engagement; not resignation
Fear-avoidance loop: pain → “damage” belief → movement fear → avoidance → deconditioning / life restriction → more pain & disability.

Treatments

ApproachMain mechanism emphasized
CBTTest damage beliefs; scheduled activity; skills; mood treatment
ACTValues-guided action without needing to eliminate painful thoughts/sensations first
MindfulnessAttend to sensation without automatic alarm reaction
PRTReframe selected primary chronic pain as safe brain-generated signaling + non-alarmed exposure
EAETApproach and express avoided emotion/trauma-related material
Evidence rule: a larger reported percentage does not automatically mean a better treatment. Narrower samples often produce bigger effects; read the population before the number.
Referral language: validate pain, name the mechanism, and offer a targeted treatment. Do not frame normal tests as evidence that pain is unreal.
Last 30 sec: pain ≠ nociception • nociceptive / neuropathic / nociplastic • PCS catastrophizing • TSK kinesiophobia • PSEQ self-efficacy • CPAQ acceptance • graded exposure breaks fear-avoidance • CBT default evidence base • ACT values • mindfulness alarm response • PRT danger prediction • function matters more than promising zero pain.
