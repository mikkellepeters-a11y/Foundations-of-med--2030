BMR Determinants

↑ BMR in deck logic: larger body mass, more lean mass, growth/youth, higher temperature; thyroid hormone and neural output are major physiologic influences.
Raw BMR = calories/day • Normalized BMR = calories/kg/day.

Tissue Fuels

TissueFuel logic
BrainGlucose; ketones in severe deprivation.
MuscleOwn glycogen; glucose for intense activity, fatty acids at rest.
LiverStores/releases glucose, gluconeogenesis, lipoprotein processing; produces fuels for others.
AdiposeMain fat reserve; low glucose → fat release.
HeartHigh fuel demand; mainly fat, also lactate/ketones.

Leptin

FeatureLecture anchor
SourcePrimarily adipose; product of the ob gene.
TargetHypothalamus.
EffectIncrease anorexic signals, decrease orexic signals → lower food intake.
FeedbackLeptin stimulates SNS; sympathetic stimulation of adipocytes reduces leptin production/expression.
Rare genetic deficiencyCan respond dramatically to leptin replacement.
Common obesityHyperleptinemia + leptin resistance; replacement usually ineffective.

Starvation Sequence

StageFuel adaptation
Prolonged fastingLiver glycogen → gluconeogenesis; muscle increases fat use.
Extended deprivationKetogenesis rises; brain uses ketones; protein sparing becomes important.
Late starvationFat depleted → accelerated protein catabolism → loss of tissue/immune function.
Last 30 seconds: 4/4/9 • BMR + TEF + NEAT + EAT • thyroid strongly affects BMR • brain glucose→ketones in deprivation • BMI = kg/m² • common obesity = high leptin/resistance • starvation ends in essential-protein loss and immune failure.
