Master framework: fuel oxidation → TCA → NADH/FADH₂ → ETC → H⁺ gradient → ATP synthase. TCA is amphibolic: energy production + biosynthetic precursors.

Catabolism, Anabolism & Coupling

Exergonic / catabolic
ΔG° negative; releases usable energy.
Endergonic / anabolic
ΔG° positive; requires energy input. Favorable reactions can drive unfavorable ones by coupling.

ETC → ATP

StepLecture anchor
1NADH/FADH₂ donate electrons to the ETC in the inner mitochondrial membrane.
2Electron transfer is ultimately to O₂.
3Energy from electron flow pumps H⁺ and creates the proton-motive/electrochemical gradient.
4H⁺ returns through ATP synthase / Complex V → ATP.
Tight coupling: ETC builds the gradient; oxidative phosphorylation captures that gradient as ATP.
