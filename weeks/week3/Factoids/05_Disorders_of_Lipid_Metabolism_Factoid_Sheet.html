Disorders of Lipid Metabolism - Factoid Sheet

Week 3 • Tuesday Lecture 5 • current deck primary
Master map: fed → fatty-acid synthesis/storage • fasting → lipolysis/carnitine shuttle/β-oxidation • malonyl-CoA prevents futile cycling • defects cause hypoketotic hypoglycemia or specialized FA accumulation • sustained oxidation → ketogenesis.

Fatty-Acid Synthesis

StepHigh yield
Citrate shuttleMoves acetyl units to cytosol.
ACCAcetyl-CoA → malonyl-CoA; rate-limiting; biotin + ATP.
Fatty-acid synthaseAdds 2-C units → palmitate.
ACC regulationInsulin/dephosphorylation + citrate activate; glucagon/epinephrine/phosphorylation + palmitoyl-CoA inhibit.
Essential FAs: linoleic (ω-6) and linolenic (ω-3); humans cannot introduce double bonds beyond C9.

Mobilization & β-Oxidation

ProcessAnchor
LipolysisATGL begins TAG→DAG; HSL and MGL continue breakdown.
Long-chain mitochondrial entryCarnitine shuttle; CPT-I is inhibited by malonyl-CoA.
β-oxidationOxidation → hydration → oxidation → thiolysis; chain shortens by 2C.
First enzymeChain-length-specific acyl-CoA dehydrogenases (SCAD/MCAD/VLCAD).
Reciprocal control: synthesis ON → malonyl-CoA high → CPT-I OFF → oxidation OFF.

Lipid Metabolism — Ketones

Ketone Bodies

ConceptHigh yield
KetonesAcetoacetate • β-hydroxybutyrate • acetone.
WhenSustained FA oxidation / fasting-starvation and other low-insulin states.
Where madeLiver mitochondria.
Rate-limiting enzymeHMG-CoA synthase (secondary AI-note clarification consistent with deck pathway).
UseExtrahepatic tissues convert ketones back to acetyl-CoA for TCA.
Liver limitationLacks thiophorase → cannot use its own ketones.
