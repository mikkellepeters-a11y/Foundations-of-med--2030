Albumin

  • Most abundant plasma protein; major source of oncotic pressure.
  • Transports hydrophobic ligands, drugs, bilirubin, fatty acids, hormones, metals.
  • Low albumin → edema risk + low total Ca with potentially normal ionized Ca.
  • A/G ratio lecture range ≈ 1.2–1.4; pattern is nonspecific.

Marker-Protein Logic

  • Cell injury releases intracellular proteins.
  • Timing depends on molecular size, cellular compartment, injury burden, and clearance.
  • Damage marker ≠ organ function test.
