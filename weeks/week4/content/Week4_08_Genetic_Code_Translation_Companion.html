DNA Transcription & Translation
Slides 4-5The genetic code converts nucleotide sequence into amino-acid sequenceLO 1

The code is read in non-overlapping triplet codons on mRNA. It is degenerate because most amino acids have more than one codon, but it is not ambiguous: each codon specifies one amino acid or stop signal.

Start

AUG establishes the reading frame and encodes methionine; the initiator tRNA is specialized for initiation.

Stop

UAA, UAG, UGA do not bind tRNAs. Release factors recognize them and terminate synthesis.

Direction

Ribosome reads mRNA 5' to 3'; the polypeptide grows from N-terminus to C-terminus.

Code logic: synonymous codons are possible because degeneracy is concentrated at the third codon position, where wobble pairing permits one tRNA to recognize more than one codon.

Slides 11-17tRNA is the adaptor; aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase is the true translatorLO 2
Amino acid + tRNA->aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase + ATP->charged aminoacyl-tRNA->ribosome

Aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases attach the correct amino acid to the correct cognate tRNA. This charging reaction uses ATP and provides much of translation's fidelity.

Wobble: relaxed base pairing between the 3rd nucleotide of the codon and the 1st nucleotide of the anticodon allows the cell to translate 61 sense codons with fewer distinct tRNAs.

Slides 22-23Elongation cycles through A, P, and E sites; release factors terminate at stop codonsLO 2
AA-tRNA enters A site->peptide bond forms->ribosome translocates->deacylated tRNA exits E site

Peptidyl transferase activity resides in ribosomal rRNA - the ribosome is a ribozyme. At a stop codon, release factors enter instead of a tRNA and promote hydrolysis of the completed polypeptide.

Slides 24-27Eukaryotic translation is regulated at initiation and at specific mRNAsLO 2

eIF2

Phosphorylation of eIF2alpha inhibits GDP-to-GTP recycling through eIF2B and suppresses global initiation. The deck uses heme-regulated control of globin synthesis as a model.

eIF4E

4E-BP can sequester cap-binding eIF4E. Phosphorylation of 4E-BP releases eIF4E and promotes cap-dependent translation.

Iron-response elements (IREs)

At low iron, IRP binds IREs: 5' UTR binding inhibits translation of ferritin/ALA synthase, while 3' UTR binding stabilizes transferrin-receptor mRNA. At high iron, IRP binds iron and releases the RNA: storage proteins rise while transferrin-receptor mRNA becomes less stable.

Low Fe: store less, import more. High Fe: store more, import less.
Slides 28-29Viruses redirect host ribosomes toward viral RNAsLO 3
IRES strategy: an internal ribosome entry site can recruit translation machinery without normal 5' cap-dependent initiation. This gives some viral RNAs a way to keep being translated while host cap-dependent synthesis is suppressed.
Core concept: viruses do not bring a separate human-style translation system. They exploit host ribosomes and initiation machinery, then alter the rules to favor viral transcripts.
Slides 30-32New polypeptides must fold, modify, and reach the correct compartmentLO 4

Free cytosolic ribosomes

Make proteins that remain in cytosol or are imported post-translationally into nucleus, mitochondria, peroxisomes, and related destinations.

Rough ER-bound ribosomes

Make secreted proteins, lysosomal proteins, and many membrane proteins through co-translational ER import.

Chaperones/chaperonins help nascent proteins acquire the correct conformation. Post-translational processing can include cleavage, covalent modification, glycosylation, phosphorylation, acetylation, and trafficking driven by signal sequences.

Signal-sequence handoff: a hydrophobic N-terminal signal can direct a translating ribosome to the ER. This sets up Wednesday Lecture 9, where the trafficking machinery is developed in detail.
Exam trapsProtect code, initiation, and regulation relationshipsAll LOs
Trap 1: Wobble is codon-anticodon flexibility, not an error in the genetic code.
Trap 2: Shine-Dalgarno = bacteria; Kozak = eukaryotes.
Trap 3: A frameshift requires an insertion/deletion not divisible by 3.
Trap 4: eIF2 phosphorylation suppresses initiation; 4E-BP binding suppresses eIF4E.
Trap 5: IRES permits cap-independent translation; it does not create a new ribosome.
30-second finish
AUG starts; UAA/UAG/UGA stop. Synthetases charge tRNAs. Shine-Dalgarno finds AUG in bacteria; Kozak helps eukaryotes. A/P/E sites drive elongation. eIF2-P turns global initiation down. IRE/IRP ties translation to iron. IRES lets viral RNA bypass cap dependence.
