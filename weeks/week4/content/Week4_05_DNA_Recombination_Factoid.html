Homologous Recombination

DSB → resection → 3' strand invasion → Holliday junction → branch migration → resolution. Spo11 creates programmed meiotic DSBs.
Consequences
Crossovers, gene conversion, new alleles, accurate DSB repair.
Failure
Nondisjunction/aneuploidy; infertility with Spo11 defects; NAHR structural rearrangements.

Linkage / HLA

Closer loci recombine less often. HLA genes are tightly linked and generally inherited as haplotypes. A full sibling has a classic 25% chance of inheriting the same two parental HLA haplotypes.

NHEJ Clinical Anchors

CML: t(9;22) BCR-ABL. Burkitt lymphoma: t(8;14) MYC. NHEJ defects can impair V(D)J recombination and cause severe immunodeficiency.

Processed Pseudogene

Cellular mRNA accidentally reverse transcribed and reinserted. Typical clues: no introns, lacks original promoter, may have 3' poly(A)-derived tract, may have target-site duplications, often nonfunctional.

Antibody Diversity

RAG1/RAG2 recognize RSS and create DSBs. NHEJ rejoins coding ends. Junctional imprecision adds diversity. Cleavage chemistry resembles ancient cut-and-paste transposition.

Repetitive DNA Disease Logic

Repeated homologous sequences can misalign → non-allelic HR → deletion, duplication, inversion, translocation. Deck anchors include PMP22 dosage disease and factor VIII inversion.

Must-Not-Miss Traps

HR needs homology; NHEJ does not.
HR is repair + meiotic exchange, not just crossover.
LINE autonomous; Alu non-autonomous.
Processed pseudogenes lack introns because the intermediate was spliced mRNA.

30-Second Review

HR = homology/high fidelity. NHEJ = direct/error prone. HLA = tight linkage. Repeats = NAHR risk. Retrotransposons use RNA + RT. RAG cleavage + NHEJ joining = V(D)J diversity.
