Cell Birth, Cell Death & Tissue Homeostasis - Factoid Sheet

Week 4 - Wednesday - Core Lecture 10 - current faculty deck + official Week 4 objectives
Master chain: growth signal → Rb restriction point → DNA-damage p53 checkpoint → repair arrest or apoptosis → tissue homeostasis.

Rb Restriction Point

Active/hypophosphorylated Rb binds E2F → S-phase genes OFF. Growth factor → Cyclin D-CDK4/6 → Rb phosphorylation/inactivation → E2F released → S phase.
Rb is a tumor suppressor. Loss of both functional alleles removes the brake; excess Cyclin D/CDK4/6 can functionally inactivate Rb. CDK4/6 inhibitors exploit this pathway.

p53 Decision

Lower damage: p53 → p21 → CDK inhibition → growth arrest/repair.
High/persistent damage: p53 → Bax → mitochondrial apoptosis.

Apoptosis - Fast Recall

Intrinsic Pathway

Stress/p53 → Bax/Bak → mitochondrial permeabilization → cytochrome c → caspase-9 → executioner caspases 3/6/7.
Anti-apoptoticPro-apoptotic

Extrinsic Pathway

FasL/death ligand → Fas/death receptor + FADD → caspase-8/10 → executioners. Caspase-8 can cleave Bid → tBid → mitochondrial amplification.

Apoptosis vs Necrosis

Apoptosis: shrinkage, chromatin condensation, intact membrane until late, PS outside, apoptotic bodies.
Necrosis: swelling, early membrane rupture, leakage, inflammation, no classic DNA ladder.

Must-Not-Miss

Rb ON = E2F OFF. G1 2N; G2/M 4N. p53-p21 = arrest. p53-Bax = death. Intrinsic = caspase-9. Extrinsic = caspase-8/10. Bcl-2 blocks death; Bax/Bak promote it. Annexin V = PS.
