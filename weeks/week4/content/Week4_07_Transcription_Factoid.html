Transcription / Processing - Fast Recall

5' Cap

7-methylguanosine. Functions: nuclear export, protection from degradation, promotion of translation.

Splicing

Spliceosome = snRNPs + proteins. Key intron landmarks: 5' donor, branch site, 3' acceptor. Alternative splicing changes exon combinations.

Polyadenylation

AAUAAA signal → cleavage ~10-30 nt downstream → poly(A) polymerase adds ~80-250 A residues. Tail supports termination, export, stability, translation.

Alternative Polyadenylation

Different 3' cleavage sites change 3' UTR length. Shorter 3' UTR can remove miRNA sites → more translation; longer 3' UTR can retain more regulatory sites.
