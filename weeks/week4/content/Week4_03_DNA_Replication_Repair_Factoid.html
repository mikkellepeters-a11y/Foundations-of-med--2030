Telomeres

End-replication problem: terminal RNA primer removal leaves no upstream 3'-OH. Telomerase = RNA-dependent DNA polymerase; RNA supplies template. Low in most somatic cells; active in stem cells and many cancers.

DNA Repair - Fast Recall

General Repair Sequence

Recognize lesion → nick/remove damaged segment when required → DNA polymerase fills gap → DNA ligase seals backbone. Direct reversal is the important exception because the lesion is chemically reversed rather than excised.

MGMT Detail

MGMT removes O6-alkylguanine damage by transferring the alkyl group to a cysteine on itself. The enzyme is permanently inactivated ("suicide" transfer). High tumor MGMT can confer resistance to alkylating chemotherapy.

Disease Anchors

MMR defect → microsatellite instability → colorectal/endometrial + other cancers.
Xeroderma pigmentosum

Cockayne syndrome
Transcription-coupled NER defect.
BRCA1/2 dysfunction
Reduced HR of double-strand breaks.

Ames Test

His- Salmonella + test chemical on histidine-free medium. More revertant colonies = stronger mutagenic signal.

Tumor-Suppressor Logic

Repair-gene loss → mutation rate rises → genome instability → oncogenic + tumor-suppressor mutations accumulate. DNA-repair genes suppress cancer by preserving genome integrity.

Must-Not-Miss Traps

BER = bad base.
NER = bulky distortion.
MMR = replication mismatch.
HR/NHEJ = DSB.

30-Second Review

5' to 3' synthesis; 3' to 5' proofread. Topo I = SSB; topo II = DSB. Telomerase copies RNA into DNA. UV → dimer → NER. Lynch → MMR. BRCA → HR. Repair loss creates a mutator phenotype and cancer risk.
