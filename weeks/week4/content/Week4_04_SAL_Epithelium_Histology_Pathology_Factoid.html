Master workflow: orient low power → classify architecture → inspect cells → relate to basement membrane/stroma → decide normal adaptation vs dysplasia/invasion.

Microscope + Digital Slide Rules

Microscope: start lowest objective, center/focus, then increase magnification. Fine focus at high power. Higher power = smaller field/depth.
Proscia: use minimap/thumbnail, zoom controls, annotation toggle/text. Start with architecture before cellular detail.

Specimen → Slide

Fixation (often buffered formalin) → gross exam/tissue selection → dehydration → clearing → paraffin infiltration → embedding → microtome sectioning (~4-10 μm in deck) → staining.

SAL Fast Recall

Basement Membrane

Anchors/supports epithelium, organizes polarity, separates it from connective tissue. Thin eosinophilic line may be seen; PAS can accentuate it; EM resolves ultrastructure. Invasive carcinoma breaches it.

Pathology Language

Hypertrophy = ↑ cell size
Atrophy = ↓ cell size
Hyperplasia = ↑ cell number
Metaplasia = mature cell type replaced by another
Dysplasia = disordered atypical growth
Pleomorphism = variable size/shape
Anaplasia = loss of differentiation
High N:C = large nucleus, scant cytoplasm

Case Anchors

Cervix: colposcopy + acetowhite change; LEEP excises lesion. LSIL = low-grade; HSIL = high-grade squamous intraepithelial lesion.
Colon: adenomas are dysplastic/premalignant and often polypoid; colonoscopy can diagnose and remove them before invasion.

Must-Not-Miss

Simple vs stratified = layers. Pseudostratified still has every cell on BM. H&E = nuclei blue-purple / protein pink. Dense regular = parallel. Dysplasia ≠ invasion. Hypertrophy = bigger; hyperplasia = more. Start low power and think in 3D.
