A/P/E

Translation Regulation - Fast Recall

Global Initiation Control

eIF2alpha-P traps limited eIF2B → GDP cannot recycle to GTP → initiation falls.
4E-BP binds eIF4E and blocks cap-dependent initiation; phosphorylation of 4E-BP releases eIF4E.

Iron Response

Iron5' IRE: Ferritin / ALA synthase3' IRE: Transferrin receptor
LowIRP binds → translation OFFIRP binds → mRNA stabilized
HighIRP releases → translation ONIRP releases → mRNA degraded more readily

Viruses

IRES = internal ribosome entry site; permits cap-independent recruitment of host translation machinery.

Protein Destination

Free ribosome: cytosolic proteins + many post-translational imports.
RER-bound ribosome: secreted, lysosomal, and membrane proteins.

Post-Translational

Chaperones help folding; proteins may undergo cleavage, glycosylation, phosphorylation, acetylation, and signal-sequence-directed trafficking.

Must-Not-Miss

Shine-Dalgarno = prok. Kozak = euk. eIF2 phosphorylation shuts initiation down. Low iron = less ferritin, more transferrin receptor. IRES = cap-independent viral translation.
