Prokaryotic DNA

Circular + negatively supercoiled + relatively naked. Gyrase = bacterial type II topoisomerase; transient DSB + ATP. Ciprofloxacin/novobiocin block gyrase resealing → DSB accumulation.

mRNA contrast
Euk: cap + poly(A), usually monocistronic. Prok: lacks those classic euk processing features, commonly polycistronic.

Must-Not-Miss Traps

High stringency = high temp + low salt.
Hybridization can bind related sequences; poor default tool for a single-base mutation.
Histone methylation is context specific; do not use the simple "methyl closes" shortcut for histone tails.
Lys acetylation neutralizes positive charge and loosens DNA binding.

Drug / Disease Anchors

Decitabine → DNMT inhibitor.
Valproic acid → HDAC inhibitor in deck.

30-Second Review

Tm rises with GC. Gyrase supercoils bacterial DNA. Telomeres/repeats characterize eukaryotic chromosomes. Euchromatin open; heterochromatin closed. DNMT closes; HAT opens; HDAC closes. tRNA adapts; rRNA catalyzes; mRNA encodes; Xist silences; miRNA represses.
