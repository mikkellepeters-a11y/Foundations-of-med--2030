Official Week 4 Objectives

1. Define signal sequence and describe how signal sequences sort proteins to cellular organelles.
2. Describe ER protein-folding quality control and the fate of misfolded proteins.
3. Describe the molecular mechanism of vesicle docking and membrane fusion.
4. Compare constitutive and regulated secretory pathways from Golgi to plasma membrane.
5. Explain cargo transport into cells and between organelles, the roles of coat proteins, and diseases caused by trafficking defects.

Endoplasmic Reticulum & Golgi Apparatus
Slides 15-20Signal sequences are molecular zip codesLO 1

Eukaryotic cells are highly compartmentalized, so hydrophilic macromolecules cannot simply diffuse through lipid bilayers. Many proteins carry signal sequences that are recognized by receptors and direct them to the correct organelle.

Slides 21-24SRP couples translation to import into the rough ERLO 1
hydrophobic signal emerges->SRP binds signal + ribosome->translation pauses->SRP receptor docks at ER->translocon opens->translation resumes into ER

SRP is a ribonucleoprotein and GTP-binding factor. Signal peptidase can cleave the N-terminal signal peptide. Membrane proteins can use start-transfer and stop-transfer sequences to establish topology.

ER modifications: N-linked glycosylation begins in the ER. Oligosaccharyl transferase adds an oligosaccharide to asparagine residues in the ER lumen, helping folding and quality control.
Slides 25-27The ER tests conformation before allowing cargo to leaveLO 2
fold with chaperones->glycan trimming acts as timer->persistent misfolding->retrotranslocation->ubiquitin->proteasome

Accumulation of unfolded proteins activates the unfolded protein response (UPR): global protein synthesis is reduced while chaperone capacity rises. If stress remains unresolved, prolonged UPR signaling can drive apoptosis.

CFTR preview: the common F508del protein has residual channel capability but misfolds enough to be retained/degraded by ER quality control, so trafficking failure becomes the disease mechanism.

Slides 30-31Rab proteins specify the target; SNAREs drive membrane fusionLO 3
vesicle Rab->target Rab effector->v-SNARE pairs with t-SNARE->SNARE zippering expels water->fusion

After fusion, NSF uses ATP to disassemble the tightly wound SNARE complex so components can be recycled.

Docking vs fusion: Rab/Rab-effector provides identity and docking specificity; complementary SNAREs provide the mechanical fusion step.

Cystic fibrosis trafficking logic

CFTR is an ATP-gated chloride/bicarbonate channel that must traverse ER and Golgi to reach the apical membrane. F508del disrupts folding; ER quality control removes much of the mutant protein before it reaches the surface. Correctors improve folding/trafficking, while potentiators improve channel function.

Slides 40-45Endocytosis brings cargo inward and uses pH to unload receptorsLO 5

As cargo moves plasma membrane → early endosome → late endosome → lysosome, pH falls. Many receptors bind ligand at neutral pH and release it after acidification; many receptors then recycle to the cell surface.

LDL

LDL receptor recruits adaptor + clathrin. Dynamin pinches off the vesicle. LDL is released in endosome; receptor recycles; cholesterol reaches lysosome. Defects can cause familial hypercholesterolemia.

EGF

EGF receptor is intentionally degraded after internalization, helping terminate a potent growth signal.

Pathogens/toxins

Viruses and toxins can exploit receptor-mediated endocytosis. The deck uses SARS-CoV-2 and botulinum toxin as trafficking examples.

Receptor fate is cargo-specific: LDL receptor is recycled; EGFR is often sent for degradation.
Slides 46-47Phagocytosis and autophagy route large cargo to lysosomal degradationLO 5

Phagocytosis

Specialized immune cells such as neutrophils and macrophages ingest large particles/pathogens, often using antibody or complement opsonization as recognition signals.

Autophagy

Cellular components are enclosed and ultimately delivered to lysosomes. It rises with stress/nutrient limitation but also contributes to normal basal housekeeping.

Exam trapsProtect route, coat, and disease relationshipsAll LOs
Trap 1: COPII = ER to Golgi; COPI = Golgi back to ER.
Trap 2: Rab specifies docking; SNARE pairing drives fusion.
Trap 3: Dynamin is a GTPase used in scission of clathrin-coated pits.
Trap 4: I-cell disease is a sorting/tagging defect, not simultaneous mutation of every hydrolase gene.
Trap 5: F508del CFTR is primarily a folding/trafficking problem before it is a surface-channel problem.
30-second finish
Signal sequence = zip code. SRP docks translating ribosome at ER. Misfolded ER proteins go to ubiquitin/proteasome; overload triggers UPR. COPII out, COPI back, clathrin inward. Rab docks, SNARE fuses. M6P sends hydrolases to lysosome. Constitutive = default; regulated = stimulus.
