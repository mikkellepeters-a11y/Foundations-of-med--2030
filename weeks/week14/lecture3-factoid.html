Papulosquamous Diseases — Factoid

MSK-Skin · Week 14 · Lecture 3 · Davis · Professor-first rapid review
Identify the rash: psoriasis = silvery, sharply demarcated; pityriasis rosea = herald patch + collarette + fir tree; lichen planus = pruritic purple polygonal planar papules + Wickham striae; seborrheic dermatitis = greasy scale (Malassezia).

Papulosquamous morphology

  • Papule/plaque + scale + well-defined margins.
  • Scale type, configuration, distribution, nail/mucosal changes separate diagnoses.

Psoriasis: overview

  • Chronic inflammatory; IL-23 / IL-12 → IL-17 T cells; IL-17, TNF-α, IFN-γ.
  • 2–4% worldwide; M = F; peaks 20–30 and 50–60; genetic predisposition + trigger.
  • Sharply demarcated erythematous plaques with silvery-white scale.

Psoriasis: distribution & nails

  • Extensor elbows/knees; scalp, low back, gluteal cleft, umbilicus, genitalia.
  • Nails: pitting, onycholysis, subungual hyperkeratosis, oil spots.
  • Nail disease → increased psoriatic arthritis risk; arthritis in ~20% of psoriasis patients (slide).

Psoriasis histology

  • Hyperkeratosis + parakeratosis, regular acanthosis, decreased granular layer.
  • Munro microabscesses: neutrophils in stratum corneum.
  • Thin suprapapillary plates + dilated papillary vessels → Auspitz (pinpoint bleeding after scale removal).
  • Koebner: new lesions at sites of trauma.

Psoriasis variants

  • Guttate: small scaly erythematous papules; strep pharyngitis association.
  • Inverse: skin creases; less prominent dry scale.
  • Pustular / erythrodermic: potentially life-threatening; slide highlights systemic corticosteroid withdrawal as common trigger.

Psoriasis therapies

  • Salicylic acid = keratolytic; tar ↓ keratinocyte proliferation.
  • Calcipotriene: vitamin D receptor → ↓ proliferation, ↑ differentiation.
  • Tazarotene: topical retinoid; topical steroids = anti-inflammatory.
  • Avoid oral corticosteroids (professor emphasis).
  • UVB / PUVA; systemic methotrexate (liver/BM), acitretin (teratogenic), cyclosporine (renal), apremilast/PDE4 (GI upset, depression).
  • Biologics: anti-TNFα, IL-17, IL-12/23, IL-23 pathways.

Pityriasis rosea

  • Ages 10–35; initial herald patch → smaller lesions.
  • Collarette of scale + along skin lines = fir-tree configuration.
  • ± itch; possible HHV-6/7 association (slide expresses uncertainty).
  • Lecture treatment options: strong topical steroid, valacyclovir, UV light.

Lichen planus

  • 5 Ps: pruritic, purple/pink (violaceous), planar, polygonal, papules.
  • Wickham striae: lacy/reticulated white lines, incl. oral/genital.
  • Hepatitis C association; Koebnerization.
  • Interface dermatitis + basal vacuolization → pigment incontinence → heals dark.
  • Topical or oral corticosteroids (lecture).

Seborrheic dermatitis

  • Greasy erythematous scale; infant cradle cap.
  • Scalp, eyebrows, nasolabial folds, ± midchest.
  • Associated with AIDS/neurologic diseases; flares with stress.
  • Malassezia → ketoconazole; zinc, selenium sulfide, tar shampoos.

Important mimics

  • Tinea: dermatophyte fungal infection; scaly plaque mimic.
  • Secondary syphilis: plantar/palmar rash; moth-eaten alopecia (slide).
  • Mycosis fungoides: cutaneous T-cell lymphoma, patch/plaque/nodular/erythrodermic presentations.
