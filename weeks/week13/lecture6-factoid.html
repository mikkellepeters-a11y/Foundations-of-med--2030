WEEK 13 · LECTURE 06

Somatic & Autonomic PNS

Foundations-style rapid-review Factoid · Dr. Mindy Johnson

1 · Vocabulary first

Afferent → toward CNS (sensory). Efferent → away from CNS (motor). Somatic → body wall/limbs. Visceral → organs. Don't conflate direction with target.

2 · Motor systems

SomaticAutonomic
1 neuron; skeletal muscle2-neuron chain; smooth/cardiac muscle and glands
No intervening ganglionic synapsePreganglionic → ganglion → postganglionic

3 · Ganglion geography

  • Paravertebral: chain beside vertebral column.
  • Prevertebral: near abdominal aorta (celiac, superior/inferior mesenteric, aorticorenal).
  • Intrinsic: near or within organ wall.

4 · Sympathetic origin

Thoracolumbar IMLCC: T1–L2/3. Fight or flight. Every preganglionic sympathetic axon enters the trunk via the same starting sequence.

5 · Rami communicantes

White = on-rampGray = off-ramp
Preganglionic into trunk; T1–L2/3 onlyPostganglionic back to spinal nerve; all spinal levels
Track it: IMLCC → ventral root → spinal nerve → ventral ramus → white ramus → trunk.

6 · Four sympathetic routes

  1. Ascend + synapse: head, neck/upper limb, cervical cardiopulmonary.
  2. Same-level synapse: middle trunk, thoracic cardiopulmonary.
  3. Descend + synapse: lower trunk and limb.
  4. Pass through WITHOUT synapse: abdominopelvic splanchnic → prevertebral ganglion → artery → visceral target.

7 · Parasympathetic outflow

Craniosacral: CN III, VII, IX, X and S2–S4. Rest and digest. COPS head ganglia: Ciliary, Otic, Pterygopalatine, Submandibular.
  • CN III/VII/IX: ganglia in head.
  • CN X: cardiac, thoracic and enteric/intrinsic ganglia.
  • S2–S4: pelvic splanchnic nerves → pelvic plexuses.

8 · Enteric two-plexus rule

Myenteric (Auerbach)Submucosal (Meissner)
Between muscle layers → peristalsisSubmucosa → secretion, water/electrolytes, local blood flow

9 · Visceral afferent routes

  • Visceral nociception often dull and poorly localized.
  • Above pelvic pain line: follow sympathetics to T1–L2 dorsal root ganglia.
  • Below pelvic pain line: follow parasympathetics to S2–S4 dorsal root ganglia.

10 · Referred pain

Visceral + somatic sensory convergence in CNS → brain misattributes visceral input to a body-wall region. Angina: chest, left upper limb, neck, jaw or back (lecture supplemental example).

FIRST AID RAPID CONNECTIONS

Supplement only: Preganglionic ANS uses ACh at nicotinic ganglionic receptors; most sympathetic postganglionic terminals use NE, except prominent ACh sweat gland pathways. Horner syndrome is a sympathetic head-pathway interruption example.

THE HIGH-YIELD DIFFERENTIATORS

White vs gray: preganglionic / limited levels vs postganglionic / all levels. Pelvic splanchnic vs abdominopelvic splanchnic: parasympathetic S2–S4 vs sympathetic fibers passing through chain. Auerbach vs Meissner: muscular contraction vs mucosal secretion.
