Thoracic + sacral: primary, concave anteriorly.
Abnormal curves
Kyphosis = exaggerated thoracic.
Lordosis = exaggerated lumbar.

FIRST AID RAPID CONNECTIONS

• Disc herniation → traversing lumbar nerve root → radiculopathy. • Pars fracture → repetitive extension in young athletes → spondylolysis. • Stenosis → narrowed neural canal/foramina → neurologic compromise. • Valve-free plexus → potential hematogenous spread.
