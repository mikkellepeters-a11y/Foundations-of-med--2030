Exam traps & quick checks

  1. Vein ≠ always deoxygenated. The pulmonary vein carries oxygenated blood.
  2. Prone ≠ posterior. Prone names the person's current posture; posterior is an anatomical direction.
  3. Biceps during lowering ≠ off. Slow descent can require eccentric biceps activation.
  4. Anastomosis ≠ guaranteed sufficient flow. A connection may be too small to compensate for sudden obstruction.
  5. Contraction ≠ necessarily movement. Isometric action has no net joint motion.
  6. Class 3 lever ≠ efficient force amplification. It favors excursion and speed.
  7. Synovial ≠ cartilaginous. Synovial joints have a cavity; cartilaginous joints unite bones directly with cartilage.
  8. Skin ≠ all vascular. Epidermis is avascular; dermis contains vessels.

Numbers / anchors: 206 adult bones (lecture), 177 engaged in movement (lecture); class 3 = most common human lever; 6 synovial joint categories; 3 broad classes of muscle and joints; torque unit N·m.

Rapid self-test

1. A horizontal CT section is which plane? Axial/transverse.

2. Fibrous tissue connecting bone to bone? Ligament.

3. What part of a long bone is its shaft? Diaphysis.

4. The atlantoaxial joint illustrates which synovial class? Pivot.

5. Active biceps lengthening with controlled lowering is? Eccentric.

6. Elbow flexion using the biceps is which class of lever? Third.

7. A blood vessel away from heart is called? Artery.

8. Thin channels returning excess tissue fluid? Lymphatic vessels.
