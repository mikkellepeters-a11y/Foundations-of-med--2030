Amino Acids, Proteins, & Enzymes - Factoid Sheet

Week 2 · Core 02 · memorize-first companion · glucose week
Core frame: R-group chemistry predicts folding. Km tells affinity; Vmax tracks functional enzyme amount. Competitive = Km up / Vmax same. Noncompetitive = Vmax down / Km same. Allosteric enzymes are sigmoidal. Isoenzymes catalyze the same reaction but differ by form/tissue.

Amino-acid classification

  • Classify by hydrophobicity, branching, charge, sulfur/OH group, nutrition, metabolic fate.
  • Essential mnemonic: PVT TIM HALL.
  • Arginine: conditionally essential when demand exceeds synthesis.
  • Histidine buffers best near physiologic pH; Glu/Asp ~4, Lys/Arg ~11 are farther away.

R groups → folding

  • Membrane-spanning alpha helices: ~20 aa; lipid-facing residues hydrophobic.
  • Proline = alpha-helix breaker.
  • Soluble proteins: hydrophobic core, hydrophilic exterior.
  • Membrane proteins: hydrophobic R groups can face lipid.
  • beta6 Glu→Val in hemoglobin = classic single-R-group change causing aggregation.

Enzyme kinetics, inhibition, regulation & isoenzymes

Km / Vmax

  • Km = [S] at 1/2 Vmax.
  • Low Km = high affinity; high Km = low affinity.
  • Km independent of enzyme concentration.
  • Vmax directly proportional to enzyme concentration.
  • [S] << Km: first-order in substrate.
  • [S] >> Km: zero-order, v = Vmax.
  • Multiple substrates: lowest Km used first.

Plots

  • Michaelis-Menten: hyperbolic.
  • Lineweaver-Burk: x-int = -1/Km; y-int = 1/Vmax.
  • Allosteric enzymes: sigmoidal.
  • Allosteric inhibitor → right shift.
  • Allosteric activator → left shift.

Clinical inhibition examples

  • Ethylene glycol / methanol: ethanol competition when fomepizole unavailable.
  • Sulfa drugs: compete with PABA-related bacterial folate synthesis.
  • Disulfiram: irreversible aldehyde dehydrogenase inhibitor.
  • Organophosphate: irreversible acetylcholinesterase inhibitor.
  • Penicillin: suicide substrate/inhibitor of transpeptidase.

Noncompetitive examples in handout

  • AMP inhibits fructose-1,6-bisphosphatase.
  • Morphine inhibits nitric oxide synthase.

Isoenzymes + CK

  • Same reaction, different molecular forms / gene products.
  • Can differ in Km, Vmax, regulation, molecular mass, mobility, pI, subunit composition.
  • Equilibrium constant, ΔG, ΔG° unchanged; activation energy may differ.
  • Tissue injury releases intracellular enzymes → serum diagnostic use.
