Newborn Screening & Genetic Counseling

Week 2 · Core 10 · Factoid Sheet · screen → confirm → act → counsel
Big distinction: a positive newborn screen is not a diagnosis. It means the infant needs timely follow-up and confirmatory testing.

NBS core

Program logic
  • Population screening before symptoms.
  • State-based.
  • Deck: screen in first 24–48 h.
  • RUSP referenced as national framework.
Lecture criteria
  • Detectable in blood within 48 h.
  • Test sufficiently sensitive/specific.
  • Condition treatable.
  • Confirmation required.
Provider follow-up
abnormal screen → contact family → assess → confirm → specialist/referral → treatment if time-sensitive → close loop
Screen vs diagnosis

Screen: risk signal. Confirmatory test: establishes or excludes diagnosis using the appropriate disease-specific test.

PKU / PAH deficiency

ConceptHigh-yield fact
InheritanceAutosomal recessive; classic disease = biallelic loss of PAH function.
Biochemistry↑ phenylalanine, relative ↓ tyrosine; neurotoxic metabolites accumulate.
Why NBS mattersNewborn can look well; neurologic injury develops with exposure and can become irreversible.
ConfirmCurrent deck emphasizes urgent plasma amino acids; same-day testing can matter.
TreatmentPhenylalanine restriction / medical food; deck also lists sapropterin and pegvaliase.
Maternal PKUPoor control can cause congenital heart defects, IUGR, microcephaly.

Variant interpretation

Three questions
  1. Variant alters protein function?
  2. Protein effect can cause phenotype?
  3. Phenotype fits patient?
Classification

Benign → likely benign → VUS → likely pathogenic → pathogenic.

VUS: uncertain is not pathogenic. Do not convert uncertainty into a definitive diagnosis or recurrence statement.

Counseling & referral - last pass

Objective bridge: the official LO requires informed, non-coercive, values-based, culturally respectful counseling. The current deck is sparse on this process, so this section is reinforced by the matched prior-year note.

Genetic counseling workflow

1. Gather

Medical history, family history/pedigree, prior testing, patient question.

2. Assess risk

Inheritance, recurrence, uncertainty, who else in family may be affected.

3. Educate

Natural history, testing options, possible results, limitations, management and reproductive implications.

4. Support

Help patient/family make the decision that fits their own values and circumstances.

Communication rules

DoAvoid
Present balanced options; check understanding; use plain language; support autonomy; use interpreters/resources when needed.Telling the patient what they “should” choose; assuming cultural/reproductive priorities; presenting uncertain results as certain.
Informed + non-coercive + values-based + culturally respectful

Referral map

Clinical genetics / genetic counselor

Diagnostic clarification, testing strategy, result interpretation, family risk.

Metabolic specialist

Time-sensitive biochemical disorders and diet/therapy planning.

Disease support groups

Education, lived-experience support, condition-specific resources.

Community resources

Practical, psychosocial, developmental, financial, and culturally appropriate support.

Fast question logic

abnormal NBS? → confirm urgently, do not wait for symptoms
VUS? → communicate uncertainty, do not overcall
genetic decision? → explain options + risks + limits, then support patient's values
positive/high-risk finding? → referral + family implications + follow-up plan
