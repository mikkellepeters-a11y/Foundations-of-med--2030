Membrane Transport - Factoid Sheet

Week 2 · Core 03 · current lecture scope
Master frame: polarized intestine/kidney cells usually use active/concentrative transport on the apical side and facilitative/equilibrative transport basolaterally. Na+/K+-ATPase on the basolateral membrane maintains the Na+ gradient that powers SGLTs.

SGLT1

  • Primarily intestine; minor kidney role.
  • Glucose + galactose.
  • Higher affinity.
  • Defect → glucose-galactose malabsorption → osmotic diarrhea.

SGLT2

  • Primarily kidney tubules; major glucose reabsorption.
  • Glucose only.
  • Lower affinity / higher renal capacity.
  • Inhibitors → glucosuria; handout flags genital yeast/UTI risk.

Km logic

Low Km = high affinity. GLUT1/3 work efficiently at low glucose. GLUT2 is low affinity, high capacity, and remains sub-saturated near usual plasma glucose.

Energy logic

SGLTs = secondary active. They do not hydrolyze ATP directly, but depend on the Na+ gradient produced by ATP-consuming Na+/K+-ATPase.

GLUT1 + FDG-PET

  • GLUT1 deficiency: early seizures, developmental delay, hypotonia/movement disorder; hypoglycorrhachia with normal blood glucose; ketogenic diet.
  • Cancers can overexpress GLUT1. FDG enters via GLUTs, is phosphorylated, cannot be further metabolized, and becomes trapped.
  • Fasting improves PET contrast; current handout says PET usually not done when blood glucose >200 mg/dL.
β-cell sequence: ↑ glucose → GLUT2 entry → ↑ ATP → ATP-sensitive K+ channel closes → depolarization (-70 → about -20 mV) → voltage-gated Ca2+ channel opens → Ca2+ influx → insulin exocytosis.

Diabetes + amino-acid transport

Sulfonylureas

Glipizide/glyburide inhibit the ATP-sensitive K+ channel → ↓ K+ outflow → depolarization → Ca2+ influx → insulin release.

GLUT4 + exercise

GLUT4 is in striated muscle/adipose. Insulin recruits preformed GLUT4 to membrane. Exercise also recruits GLUT4 independently of insulin.

GLUT4 clinical logic

  • Insulin-responsive tissues named: skeletal muscle, cardiac muscle, adipose; not liver.
  • Exercise increases glucose uptake in skeletal muscle and can help manage postprandial glucose.
  • Handout warns about exercise-related hypoglycemia, especially in type 1 diabetes treated with insulin.

GLUT5

Apical intestinal fructose uptake; fructose exits mucosal cell by GLUT2. Defect → fructose malabsorption.

Amino-acid transport

>20 transporters; multiple routes per amino acid. External absorption/reabsorption generally secondary active; internal redistribution generally facilitative/exchange.

Dietary protein detail: current handout says ~70–80% is absorbed as di- or tri-peptides, not as individual amino acids.

Do not confuse

Cystinuria = membrane transport problem → urine cystine → stones. Cystinosis = lysosomal transporter problem → cystine accumulates inside cells.

Last-minute discriminators

  1. SGLT1 = intestine / glucose + galactose; SGLT2 = kidney / glucose.
  2. GLUT1 = BBB/basal; GLUT2 = sensor/high capacity; GLUT4 = insulin + exercise; GLUT5 = fructose.
  3. FDG is trapped after phosphorylation because it cannot proceed through metabolism.
  4. Sulfonylurea target is the ATP-sensitive K+ channel, not GLUT2.
  5. Hartnup = neutral AA / tryptophan-niacin; cystinuria = stones; cystinosis = lysosomes.
