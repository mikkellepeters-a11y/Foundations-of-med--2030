Article + evidence structure

Article: Title → Abstract → Introduction → Methods → Results → Discussion → Conclusion.
Meta-analysis: quantitative synthesis of existing evidence; a “study of studies” used to guide research and investigate heterogeneity.
Acquire: pre-appraised evidence, textbooks (noted as becoming outdated quickly), search engines/PubMed.

Core stats

Type I error
Reject H0 when true.
α = probability Type I.
Type II error
Lecture recap: “accept” H0 when false.
β = probability Type II.
Power
1 − β. Lecture figure: α .05, β .20, power .80.
Clinical vs statistical significance
Sample size and effect size matter.
Misleading conclusions: biased samples · overgeneralization · causality · wrong analysis · violated assumptions · data mining.

EBM / Biostats - Calculations & Validity

Week 2 rapid calculation page

Risk vs odds

Risk = successes / total outcomes.
Odds = successes / failures.

Relative Risk vs Odds Ratio

Relative Risk (RR)Odds Ratio (OR)
Formula[a/(a+b)] / [c/(c+d)](a/c) / (b/d)
Lecture useProspective studies where incidence is tracked; not case-control/cross-sectionalCase-control + cross-sectional; recap says OR may also be used in cohort
MeaningRisk in exposed relative to unexposedOdds of exposure in disease group relative to non-disease group
ExampleRadiation cohort: RR = 6.51Sweetener case-control: OR = 1.025; p=.592; 95% CI .94–1.12

Diagnostic test 2×2

MetricLecture definitionRecall cue
SensitivityDiseased people correctly identifiedHigh = good screening; 100% → no false negatives
SpecificityNon-diseased people correctly identifiedHigh = good confirmation; 100% → no false positives
PPVAmong positive tests, proportion with diseaseCondition on test-positive row
NPVAmong negative tests, proportion without diseaseCondition on test-negative row
Lecture example: sensitivity 80% · specificity 89% · PPV 44% · NPV 98%.

Reliability vs validity

Reliability = precision
Lack of random error; consistency/reproducibility.
Validity = accuracy
Lack of systematic error; closeness to true value; assessed against a gold standard.

Ethics

IRB oversight · minimize and justify risk · informed consent · protect participant privacy.
Fast pathway: identify design → choose RR/OR using lecture rules → read 2×2 orientation → separate reliability (precision) from validity (accuracy).
