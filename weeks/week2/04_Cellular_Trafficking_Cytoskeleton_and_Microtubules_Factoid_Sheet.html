Microtubule build

  • Basic unit = α/β-tubulin heterodimer.
  • Head-to-tail assembly creates polarity.
  • Usually 13 protofilaments form hollow tube.
  • Plus end = terminal β-tubulin.
  • Minus end = terminal α-tubulin.

Nucleation

  • MTOCs overcome assembly barrier.
  • γ-tubulin ring complex nucleates microtubules.
  • Centrosome contains many MTOCs and is often near nucleus.
  • Specialized cells can use non-centrosomal sites.

Dynamic instability

StateWhat is happening?
GrowthGTP-tubulin adds; distal GTP cap stabilizes growing end.
CatastropheGTP cap lost → GDP-tubulin exposed → rapid depolymerization.
RescueSwitch from shrinkage back to growth.
RegulationEnd-binding proteins, MAPs and tubulin post-translational modifications tune stability and motor traffic.
Direction anchor: most kinesin = PLUS-end traffic. Cytoplasmic dynein = MINUS-end traffic.

Kinesin

  • ATP-binding motor.
  • Usually plus-end directed.
  • Dimeric stepping: alternating ATP binding/hydrolysis drives conformational change and movement.
  • Especially important for outward/anterograde cargo transport.

Dynein

  • Cytoplasmic dynein = most minus-end cargo traffic.
  • Large multisubunit motor.
  • Needs regulators such as dynactin, Lis1 and cargo adaptors.
  • Axonemal dynein = ciliary/flagellar bending.

Ciliopathies

  • Primary ciliary dyskinesia: recurrent sinus/bronchial/ear infections, mucus buildup, infertility.
  • Kartagener: situs inversus + sinusitis + bronchiectasis.
  • PKD: primary-cilium proteins; disrupted fluid-flow sensing / Ca2+ signaling framework.
  • Bardet-Biedl: kidney disease/cysts, vision loss, polydactyly, weight gain; primary-cilium/BBSome connection.

Microtubules: disease + therapy

Why neurons are vulnerable

Axons are long, thin and highly polarized. Diffusion is inadequate for long-distance transport, so subtle microtubule or motor defects can create severe cargo “traffic jams.”

DisorderKey microtubule/motor connection
LissencephalyDefective neuronal migration; Lis1 is a dynein regulator. Loss of both Lis1 copies is lethal in the lecture framework.

Regulation pearl: motor disease is not always “motor stops.” The lecture explicitly emphasizes that unregulated hyperactivity is also harmful.

Fast discriminators

  1. β at plus end, α at minus end.
  2. γ-tubulin nucleates; α/β-tubulin builds the track.
  3. GTP cap lost = catastrophe.
  4. Kinesin → plus; cytoplasmic dynein → minus.
  5. Axonemal dynein bends 9+2 cilia; primary cilia are 9+0 sensors.
  6. Kartagener = motile cilia; PKD/BBS = primary cilia.
  7. Tau = MAP; NFTs are intracellular.
  8. Vinblastine perturbs/destabilizes dynamics; paclitaxel stabilizes. Both can arrest mitosis.
