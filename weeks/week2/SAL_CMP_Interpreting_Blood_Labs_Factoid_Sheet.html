Interpreting Blood Labs - CMP - Factoid Sheet

Week 2 SAL · memorize-first companion · current lecture scope
Core frame: Do not diagnose from one red number. Ask why the test was ordered, whether the timing/method is appropriate, and whether the pattern fits the patient.

BMP - 8

Glucose, calcium, sodium, potassium, CO2, chloride, BUN, creatinine.

Think electrolytes + glucose/metabolic status + renal function.

CMP = BMP + 6

Albumin, total protein, bilirubin, ALP, ALT, AST.

Adds serum proteins + hepatic/biliary markers.

Reference ranges - the exam logic

FactMeaning
Central ~95%Some healthy people are outside the reported range by definition.
20+ independent testsLecture: >50% chance at least one is "abnormal" from statistical variation.
Ranges varyAge, sex, physiology, population, method/analyzer, collection/timing.
Exact values slideDo not memorize per faculty slide; learn patterns.

Overuse / misuse / underuse

  • Overuse: unnecessary daily panels -> cost + incidental findings.
  • Misuse: wrong time/test -> misleading result.
  • Underuse: missed treatable condition.

Harmonization

Same clinically acceptable result/interpretation despite method, units/reference interval, time, or place.

Examples: cholesterol, HbA1c, creatinine.

Why not all? Cost, time, operational/vendor barriers.

Newborn bilirubin

TermKnow it
Indirect / unconjugatedNot yet processed by liver; albumin-bound; high levels can be neurotoxic.
Direct / conjugatedProcessed by liver; water-soluble; ready for excretion.
Why newborns?Higher RBC turnover + immature liver.
Major dangerKernicterus.
Prevention/treatment in lecturePhototherapy converts bilirubin to a more water-soluble form.

Calcium high

Hyperparathyroidism, bone metastasis, Paget disease, granulomatous disease, excess Ca/Vit D, thiazides, prolonged bed rest.

Calcium low

Hypoparathyroidism, renal failure, Mg deficiency, pancreatitis, vitamin D deficiency.

Case drill: acute clot + protein C ordered = misuse/timing. Stable patient + reflex daily CMP/CBC = overuse. Newborn severe jaundice = underuse danger / indirect bilirubin / kernicterus / phototherapy.

Last-minute five

  1. BMP = 8; CMP = BMP + 6.
  2. Central 95% means healthy people can still be flagged.
  3. Do not memorize the entire numeric range slide.
  4. Direct bilirubin -> obstruction; ALP -> bile duct.
  5. Interpret the pattern in the patient, not the isolated lab.
