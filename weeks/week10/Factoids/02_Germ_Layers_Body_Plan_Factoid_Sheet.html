Folding of the Embryo

Why Folding Matters

Week 4 folding turns the flat disc into a cylindrical embryo. It forms the ventral body wall, contributes to gut tube formation, and repositions the heart and other structures.
TypeMain effectsAssociated defect if disrupted
Craniocaudal (head/tail)Foregut + hindgut formation; heart/septum transversum repositioning; cloacal membrane/allantois relocationCaudal closure defects, e.g. bladder exstrophy
LateralVentral body wall formation; yolk sac pinched into gut tube; body cavities establishedOmphalocele, gastroschisis

Lateral Folding

Somatic mesoderm + ectoderm
Forms ventral body wall.
Visceral mesoderm + endoderm
Forms gut wall / gut tube.
Key point: the space between somatic and visceral mesoderm becomes the intraembryonic coelom and future body cavities.
