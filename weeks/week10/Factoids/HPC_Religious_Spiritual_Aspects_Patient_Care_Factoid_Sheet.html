Religious & Spiritual Aspects of Patient Care

Week 10 · HPC · Factoid Sheet
Four objectives: define SFR • connect spiritual care to PFCC • perform a basic spiritual screen • know when/how to use chaplains.

SFR Definitions

TermLecture framing
SpiritualityHow a person — body, mind, soul — relates to others/universe; questions of identity, purpose, meaning, suffering
FaithTrust, commitment, remembrance, belief, accepting truth without proof
ReligionOrganized attempt to address existential questions, provide teaching, transmit tradition, and provide rituals

PFCC

Patient-centered
Patient is a unique person with individual needs and life history.
Clinician behavior
Kindness, respect, listening, authenticity, physical presence, timely responses, family relationships.
Spiritual care belongs in PFCC because beliefs and values may affect coping, meaning, medical decisions, and recovery.

Basic Screening History

DoAvoid
Keep it simple and straightforwardDo not push
Place in/after Social History, before ROSDo not project your own beliefs
Ask where the patient finds strengthDo not force a spiritual history
Listen and let the response guide follow-upDo not provide complex spiritual direction if untrained
Watch body language and back off if neededDo not ignore complex needs — consult chaplain

FICA

CCommunityAre you part of a spiritual/religious community? Does it support you?
AAddress in CareHow would you like us to address these issues in your care?

Actions, Boundaries & Chaplaincy

Appropriate Actions

ActionLecture examples
ListenStorytelling, journaling, discussion of spiritual life
Create spaceQuiet reflection/prayer, chapel information or transportation
FacilitateRequested rituals/practices; relevant reading/videos
IntegrateInclude relevant beliefs in the care plan
ConsultPatient’s clergy and/or hospital chaplain for complex issues

Autonomy / Boundaries

Core ethical rule: it is unethical to project your belief system onto a patient or force a spiritual history on a nonreligious patient.
If defensive: explain that you are being thorough, then back off if needed.

Chaplain Role

Chaplains = trained healthcare consultants for complex spiritual care.
Why involve them?Lecture framing
Complex spiritual distressThey are highly skilled and trained for complex spiritual care
Clinician discomfort or limitsFind someone who is comfortable and trained
Interdisciplinary careEffective emotional/spiritual care requires proactive team collaboration
