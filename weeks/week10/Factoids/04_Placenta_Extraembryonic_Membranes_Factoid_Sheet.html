Placenta & Extraembryonic/Fetal Membranes

Week 10 · Tuesday · Core Lecture 4 · Factoid Sheet
Placenta = fetal chorion frondosum + maternal decidua basalis. Maternal blood bathes villi in the intervillous space; fetal blood stays in villous capillaries.
