Hemolytic Disease of the Newborn

RhD-negative mother + RhD-positive fetus → maternal anti-RhD IgG can cross placenta in later sensitized pregnancy and destroy fetal RBCs.
PointRemember
Antibody classIgG
First pregnancyUsually sensitization rather than severe disease
Later pregnancyAnti-RhD IgG crosses placenta if fetus is RhD+
PreventionRhoGAM / anti-RhD immunoglobulin
Other systemsAB, MNS, Kidd, Duffy; Kell can cause anemia differently
Pathologic maternal IgG can cross too: anti-Ro/SSA, anti-La/SSB, pemphigus antibodies, myasthenia antibodies, ITP-related antibodies.
