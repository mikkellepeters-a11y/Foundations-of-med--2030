Specimen & Media

Collect
Acute phase, preferably before antibiotics; correct site; minimize contamination; adequate quantity.
Preserve
Package correctly, label patient + specific site, transport promptly or store to maintain viability.

Diagnostic Test Math

MetricFormulaLecture example

PPVTP/(TP+FP)67%

Prevalence: with sensitivity/specificity held constant, higher prevalence raises PPV. Lecture example: PPV ~32% at 5% prevalence → ~69% at 20%.
