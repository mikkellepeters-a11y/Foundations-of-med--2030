Umbilical Cord

FindingDefinition / association
Marginal insertionWithin 1 cm of disc edge; less efficient
Velamentous insertionInsertion in membranes; unprotected vessels → compression/disruption
Furcate insertionVessels branch before placental surface; no Wharton protection
Single umbilical arteryConfirm microscopically; associated anomalies/karyotype abnormalities
Long cord >70 cmKnots, prolapse, nuchal cord
Short cord <30 cmAbruption
ThrombosisAssociated with funisitis and true knots
Relationship: all velamentous insertions are furcate; not all furcate insertions are velamentous.

Placental Vascular / Gross Pathology

LesionKey point
Maternal-surface calcificationUsually not pathologic
InfarctMaternal/fetal vascular malperfusion; may mimic intervillous thrombus grossly
AbruptionRetroplacental hematoma adherent to basal plate; may indent maternal surface
Failed spiral-artery remodelingPersistence of smooth muscle in central decidua basalis arteries
Hydropic placentaSwollen but recognizable cotyledons; no separate cysts
Hydropic placenta associations: Rh incompatibility • TORCH • alpha thalassemia • cardiac anomalies • abnormal fetal karyotype (lecture example: Turner 45,X).

Membranes, Infection & Twins

Membrane Insertion

PatternDistinction
CircummarginateFlat fibrin ring inside disc margin
CircumvallateRaised insertion; smaller amnion because membranes do not reach periphery

Meconium

Gross
Green-brown staining of cord and membranes; amnion can appear edematous.
Microscopy
Meconium-laden macrophages in amnion, chorion, and cord stroma; deeper pigment can imply chronicity.

Amniotic Fluid Infection Sequence

Ascending infection is more common. Lecture organisms: Ureaplasma, E. coli, Fusobacterium.
ResponseStages
Maternal1 acute chorionitis/subchorionitis → 2 acute chorioamnionitis → 3 necrotizing acute chorioamnionitis
Fetal1 chorionic plate vasculitis + umbilical vein phlebitis → 2 arteritis ± phlebitis → 3 necrotizing funisitis
Funisitis = inflammation of the umbilical cord. Neutrophils are in vessel walls and may extend into Wharton jelly.
