Early Embryonic Development - Factoid Sheet

Week 10 • Monday • Core Lecture 1 • Fertilization & Implantation
Master sequence: meiosis → ovulation → capacitation → fertilization in ampulla → cleavage → morula → blastocyst → implantation → bilaminar disc + hCG.

Gametes & Meiosis

Oocyte
Primary oocyte arrests in prophase I. LH resumes meiosis I. Ovulated secondary oocyte arrests in metaphase II. Fertilization completes meiosis II.
Sperm
Head = nucleus + acrosome. Tail = motility. Only capacitated sperm can fertilize.

hCG & Early Pregnancy

hCG source = syncytiotrophoblast. It enters maternal blood through trophoblastic lacunae and is the key mechanistic link to early pregnancy testing.

Clinical Application Framework

Sperm problem?
Motility → capacitation → acrosome → zona penetration → membrane fusion.
Oocyte problem?
Ovulation → meiotic maturation → zona interaction → completion of meiosis II.
Implantation problem?
Zona shedding → trophoblast differentiation → syncytial invasion → endometrial interaction.
Pregnancy test?
Think syncytiotrophoblast → hCG.
