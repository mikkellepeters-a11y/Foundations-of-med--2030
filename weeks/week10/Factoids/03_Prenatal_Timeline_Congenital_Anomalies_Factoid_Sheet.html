Neural Tube Closure

Folate is especially important days 18–33; neural tube normally closes by end of week 4.
DefectWhat protrudes / is missing
Spina bifida occultaVertebral arch gap; skin-covered; often hair tuft
MeningoceleMeninges + CSF
MyelomeningoceleMeninges + spinal cord and/or roots
EncephaloceleBrain + meninges through skull opening
AnencephalyMajor brain/skull/scalp absent; cranial neuropore closure failure
Detection: ultrasound; amniocentesis; elevated α-fetoprotein in maternal serum/amniotic fluid for open NTDs.
