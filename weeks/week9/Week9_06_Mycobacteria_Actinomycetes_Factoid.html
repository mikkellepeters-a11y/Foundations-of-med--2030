Mycobacteria vs Nocardia

FeatureMycobacteriaNocardia (deck actinomycete)
Acid-fastnessAcid-fastWeakly / partially acid-fast
Mycolic acids70–90 carbons50–60 carbons
MorphologyAerobic bacilli/filamentsThin, branching filamentous bacterium; aerial hyphae
EnvironmentWater + soilWater, decaying organic matter, soil
HistologyGranulomatous inflammationNo granuloma; suppurative liquefaction + granulation/fibrosis
AFB stain
Carbol fuchsin → AFB remain red despite acid-alcohol. Non-AFB decolorize → methylene-blue blue.
Mycobacterial wall
Peptidoglycan + arabinogalactan + mycolic acids + lipoarabinomannan; lipid-rich/hydrophobic; difficult to stain; resistant to disinfectants.

Cell-Mediated Immunity + Granulomas

Macrophage → IL-12 + TNF-α → Th1 → IFN-γ → macrophage activation → intracellular NTM killing.
Disseminated NTM risk
AIDS with CD4 <50/µL · IFN-γ/IL-12 pathway mutations · Mendelian susceptibility involving IL-12/IFN-γ axis.
Histology
Granulomatous inflammation with multinucleated giant cells; nuclei in arcs = Langhans cells.

NTM Disease Anchors

OrganismHigh-yield deck clues
MACM. avium + M. intracellulare; slow non-chromogen; soil/water; disseminated AIDS/T-cell deficiency; fever/night sweats/weight loss; older male smokers can have cavitary TB-like disease; elderly female nonsmokers can have middle-lobe/lingular nodules + bronchiectasis; abundant AFB in macrophages in AIDS.
M. kansasiiSlow photochromogen; tap water in endemic areas; older men, smoking/COPD, prior TB/pneumoconiosis/bronchiectasis; most like TB; right-upper-lobe predilection; cavitation 90%.
M. abscessusElderly female nonsmokers; no underlying lung disease; resembles non-cavitary MAC; nodules + bronchiectasis.
M. scrofulaceumCervical lymphadenitis in previously healthy children; slow scotochromogen; swamp water; deck treatment = excision.

Leprosy = CMI Spectrum

FeatureTuberculoid (TT)Lepromatous (LL)
CMIVigorous TH1; IL-2 + IFN-γWeak TH1 + ↑TH2; weak CMI
OrganismsLow; paucibacillaryHigh; multibacillary

M. leprae basics
Not cultured in vitro · mouse footpads/armadillos · optimal 32–34°C · helper T-cell response determines outcome.
Deck treatment
Dapsone (G6PD hemolysis) + rifampin (hepatotoxicity) + clofazimine (reddish pigmentation); slide labels 2 years.

Nocardia

CategoryDeck factoids
IdentityAerobic Gram+ weakly acid-fast true bacterium; water/soil/decaying matter; branching filaments; aerial hyphae.
StainModified acid-fast / Fite-Faraco; irregular “beads” along branching filaments.
TransmissionInhalation; skin injury less common; no person-to-person; Southwest dust/wind favors aerosolization.
PathogenesisCord factor; blocks phagosome acidification + phagolysosome fusion; T-cell immunity crucial; no granuloma.
N. asteroidesOpportunistic pulmonary disease → may disseminate to CNS → cerebral abscess.
N. brasiliensisSkin infection after soil contamination → cellulitis/lymphocutaneous disease/actinomycetoma + chronic draining fistulae.
Deck treatmentSulfa drugs · meropenem · 3rd-gen cephalosporins · linezolid.

Exam Traps

Granuloma trap: NTM = granulomatous. Nocardia = no granuloma.
Pigment trap: photochromogen needs light; scotochromogen does not.
kansasii vs MAC: kansasii = photochromogen/TB-like RUL cavity; MAC = nonchromogen + multiple pulmonary patterns/AIDs dissemination.
Leprosy trap: stronger CMI = tuberculoid + fewer organisms; weak CMI = lepromatous + many organisms.
