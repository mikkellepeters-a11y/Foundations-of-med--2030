Food / Exposure Timing

B. cereus emetic
Cooked/reheated rice · heat-stable toxin · 1–5 h · vomiting.
C. perfringens food poisoning
Heat-labile pore-forming toxin · 8–24 h · watery diarrhea.
Infant botulism
Ingested spores → germination → toxin · floppy baby; deck shows honey/corn syrup.
Listeria
Foodborne exposure + pregnancy/newborn/immunocompromised → invasive disease, sepsis, meningitis.
