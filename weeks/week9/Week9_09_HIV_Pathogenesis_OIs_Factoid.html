HIV Pathogenesis & Progression

Life cycle
Binding → fusion → reverse transcription → integration → replication → assembly → budding.
CD4 + coreceptors
CD4 is the HIV binding site. Chemokine GPCR coreceptors aid entry. CCR5 Δ32: homozygous resistant; heterozygous slower progression (deck).
Early pathophys
↑ viral replication · ↓ CD4 T cells · ↓ gut barrier function.
Progression
Acute viremia / CD4 drop → partial control + chronic/asymptomatic progressive infection → late viral rebound + CD4 depletion → AIDS.
Diagnosis timing
NAT earliest → p24/Ag-Ab window → antibody responses later. Deck notes Western blot is no longer used for HIV diagnosis.
AIDS: CD4 <200 cells/mm³ OR an AIDS-defining opportunistic infection/condition.

PCP

Moderate–severe definition
Room-air PaO₂ <70 mmHg OR A–a gradient ≥35 mmHg.
Treatment
Mild: TMP-SMX. Moderate–severe: TMP-SMX + corticosteroids. G6PD testing before dapsone/primaquine.

Exam Traps

Toxo vs PML: toxo = mass lesions (often multiple/basal ganglia); PML = white-matter demyelination.
MAC prophylaxis: CD4 <50 alone is not enough in this deck; ART status matters.
PCP severe: add steroids based on PaO₂/A–a criteria, not simply CD4 count.
AIDS definition: CD4 <200 OR defining illness.
