Master frame: organisms + genes/functions → mucosal immune balance → life-course colonization → fiber/SCFAs → dysbiosis associations → autoimmunity/obesity.

Core Definitions + Numbers

Human cells: ~30 trillion
Bacterial cells: ~39 trillion
Species: ~2,000
Anaerobes dominate ~1000:1.
Up to 90%: Bacteroidetes + Firmicutes.
Microbiota = organisms.
Microbiome = organisms + genes + interactions/functions/products.

What the Microbiome Does

Ferments otherwise indigestible plant carbohydrates.
Supports fat regulation, vitamin/amino-acid synthesis, and protection from invasive microbes.
Crucial to maturation/function of innate + adaptive immunity.

SCFAs

>50% of colonic SCFA pool.
Propionate C3

Fiber fermentation → SCFAs → colonocyte energy + epithelial barrier support + Treg promotion + ↓ intestinal inflammation.

Non-Intestinal Autoimmune Associations

DiseaseHandout association
Graves / HashimotoDysbiosis/leaky gut; Graves includes anti-gliadin + anti-Saccharomyces antibodies.
Type 1 DMEnvironmental/microbial-diversity hypothesis; diverse microbiome suggested protective in animal models.
Multiple sclerosis↑ Acinetobacter + Akkermansia, ↓ Bifidobacterium; molecular mimicry discussed.
SLELower diversity; altered Firmicutes/Bacteroidetes; inflammatory dysbiosis.
Rheumatoid arthritisNew-onset RA: ↓ diversity, ↓ Clostridium, ↑ Prevotella.
EXAM CAUTION: The handout explicitly says dysbiosis is associated with multiple diseases but does not prove it causes them. Preserve association vs causation.

Obesity / Type 2 Diabetes

Obesity pattern: Firmicutes-heavy microbiome; obese-mouse flora enriched for energy-harvesting genes; obese→germ-free fecal transfer increases body fat.
T2DM pattern: ↑ Firmicutes, ↓ Bacteroidetes; high-fat diet → ↑ plasma LPS in the handout's inflammation/insulin-resistance model.

Diet

Mediterranean pattern
High fiber / plant-rich / lower saturated fat → higher SCFAs.
Western pattern
Higher saturated fat, meat, dairy → lower SCFAs; low SCFAs associated with disease.

Pre / Pro / Syn / Post

Prebiotic: non-digestible fiber supporting beneficial microbes.
Probiotic: living organisms administered for putative benefit.
Synbiotic: prebiotic + probiotic.
Postbiotic: bioactive products of probiotic fermentation.
