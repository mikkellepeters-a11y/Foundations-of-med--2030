Gram-Negative Envelope + Endotoxin

Envelope
Outer membrane + LPS + porins + thin peptidoglycan + periplasm + inner membrane.
Lipid A
Anchors LPS; N-acetylglucosamine dimer + 6 fatty acids. Toxic component → fever, ↑/↓ WBCs, shock, DIC.
Core + O side chain
Core: KDO + heptoses. O antigen: 0–50 repeating polysaccharides; unique/variable among GNR.
LPS functions: permeability barrier (including bile salts) + survival against serum bactericidal components. Endotoxin increases during rapid growth and bacterial lysis.

Enterobacteriaceae: Rules You Should Know Cold

ALL: facultative anaerobes · ferment glucose · reduce nitrate → nitrite · cytochrome c oxidase NEGATIVE. Variable: lactose fermentation.

Fimbriae + Enteric Toxins

Fimbrial tropism
GI mucosa → traveler’s diarrhea pattern
Blood-group antigens on uroepithelium → UTI
Choroid plexus → neonatal meningitis
ETEC
Heat-labile toxin → ↑ cAMP → Cl secretion → Na follows → osmotic diarrhea
Heat-stable toxin → ↑ cGMP → same downstream secretory effect
Shiga toxin: E. coli O157:H7 + Shigella dysenteriae type 1 → bloody diarrhea + HUS / microangiopathic hemolytic anemia.

MacConkey + Oxidase = Fast Sorting

MacConkey agar
Selective: bile salts + crystal violet inhibit Gram+.
Differential: lactose + neutral red.
Lactose+ → acid → pink/red; lactose− → colorless.
Cytochrome oxidase
Enterobacteriaceae = oxidase−.
Pseudomonas aeruginosa = oxidase+.
TMPPD turning blue = positive oxidase test.

Gram-Negative Anaerobes

Major groups
Bacteroides · Fusobacterium · Prevotella · Veillonella
Bacteroidetes features
Anaerobic ETC uses NO₃ rather than O₂ as final electron acceptor; contain endotoxin; many make β-lactamase.
Habitat
Anaerobes predominate at mucosal sites; deck emphasizes saliva and colon/feces.
Virulence
Capsule · fimbriae · collagenases · endotoxin · Ig proteases · resistance to O₂ toxicity via catalase/SOD.
Clinical clues
Near mucosa · mixed flora on Gram stain · malodor · tissue gas.
Anaerobic culture examples: thioglycolate broth · anaerobic jar with blood agar · cooked chopped-meat broth.

Exam Traps

Do not confuse: Lipid A = toxic + membrane anchor. O antigen = variable outer antigen. Core = KDO + heptoses.
Do not confuse: MacConkey pink/red = lactose fermentation, not oxidase status.
Pseudomonas is oxidase+ and non-fermenting; Enterobacteriaceae are oxidase− and glucose-fermenting.
Proteus stone clue comes from urease → NH₃ → alkaline urine.
