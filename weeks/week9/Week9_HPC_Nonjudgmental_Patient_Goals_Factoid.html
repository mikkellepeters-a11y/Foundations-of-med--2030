Nonjudgmental Understanding of Patient Goals & Perspectives

Week 9 · Thursday HPC · 2025 handout preview pending 2026 update
Master flow: RAPPORT → broad opening → “WHAT ELSE?” → negotiate agenda → explore perspective/context → empathize → summarize/share with ATA/ART → shared plan → MI/BAP when behavior change is needed.

Why PCC Matters

Interrupt time
Handout answer: ~11 seconds.
Emotional clues
Responding to them is associated with visits ~3 minutes shorter in the handout.
Relationship
Handout: patient-provider relationship = #1 predictor of psychotherapy outcomes; goal consensus = #2.
Patient vs provider mismatch: self-rated vs provider-rated health correlation r = 0.45. Physician ratings lean more on physical factors; patient self-ratings more on psychosocial factors.

The 3 Elements of PCC

1. Elicit patient perspective
2. Understand psychosocial context
3. Reach shared treatment goals aligned with patient values

Ending the Visit

Orient
“We have a few minutes left…”
Summarize
“Here’s what I’ve heard…” / “If I’m understanding correctly…”
Check understanding
ART / ATA loops + teach-back; can be used throughout visit

ATA / ART

Ask–Tell–Ask / Ask–Respond–Tell: learn what the patient understands or wants → respond/share information → verify the new understanding. The handout treats these as loops, not one-way information delivery.

Advanced PCC

ToolHandout framework
Motivational InterviewingProcess, style, and spirit of communication. Four parts: engaging → focusing → evoking → planning. Develop discrepancy; “roll with resistance.”
Brief Action PlanningAsk whether patient wants to act → offer behavioral menu if needed → SMART behavioral contract → elicit commitment → confidence scale 1–10 → if <7, problem-solve barriers → check progress.

Common Traps

PCC is not “being nice.”
It has defined tasks: perspective, context, and shared goals.
Do not agenda-set too early.
First cast the net wide and exhaust the list with “what else?”
Empathy is not only feeling.
The fourth quality is communicating your understanding.
MI ≠ generic advice.
The handout frames it as engaging, focusing, evoking, planning.
