Folate: TMP-SMX

MOA
Sulfamethoxazole = DHPS block. Trimethoprim = DHFR block. Sequential folate inhibition → ↓ purines/pyrimidines.
Spectrum
Strep · Staph · Enterobacterales · protozoa · Pneumocystis · Stenotrophomonas · Acinetobacter · Nocardia.
Toxicity / pharm: SJS · bone-marrow suppression · nephrotoxicity · hyperkalemia · expected SCr bump · IV hyponatremia · avoid 1st & 3rd trimesters.

Rapid Sorting / Exam Traps

Pneumonia trap: daptomycin is inactivated by lung surfactant.
FQ trap: moxi = anaerobes but no Pseudomonas / no urine.
Rifampin trap: never solo; major CYP induction.
TMP-SMX trap: think SJS + marrow + hyperK + creatinine bump.
30S: aminoglycosides + tetracyclines.
50S: macrolides + clindamycin + linezolid.
