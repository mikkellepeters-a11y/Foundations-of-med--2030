Group A Strep

M protein = major virulence factor; inhibits phagocytosis; surface coating; attachment.
Capsule = human-like hyaluronic acid camouflage.
C5a protease = complement inactivation.
Streptokinase = clot lysis/dissemination.
Streptolysin O/S = pores/tissue invasion.
Hyaluronidase/RNase/DNase = tissue spread.
Scarlet fever: Spe → strawberry tongue + blanching finely papular rash + Pastia lines. Superantigen mechanism is shown immediately after.
Post-strep: deck shows immune-complex GN with subepithelial humps; rheumatic fever with TH1-mediated valve injury; Sydenham chorea/PANDAS follow-up.
