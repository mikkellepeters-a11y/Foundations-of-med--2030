N. gonorrhoeae

Disease
Urethritis · endocervicitis · PID → scarring/infertility · DGI · ophthalmia neonatorum · proctitis · pharyngitis · arthritis.
Diagnosis
Gram stain urethral discharge · Thayer-Martin · glucose+ / maltose− / sucrose− · DNA probe.
Deck treatment/prevention: ceftriaxone; mixed Chlamydia infection → add tetracycline; nonspecific prevention (e.g., condoms). Resistance slide: β-lactamase, efflux, PenA/PenB, gyrase mutations, multidrug resistance.

N. meningitidis

Clinical
Meningitis: fever + headache + stiff neck + ↑ PMNs in CSF.
Meningococcemia → DIC + shock + Waterhouse-Friderichsen.
Diagnosis
CSF · kidney-shaped G− diplococci · latex agglutination · BAP/CHOC · oxidase+ · glucose+ · maltose+.
Risk: late complement deficiency + splenectomy. Deck treatment: ceftriaxone / high-dose PCN-G / carbapenem; chloramphenicol if allergic. Close-contact prophylaxis: rifampin; ceftriaxone in pregnancy; ciprofloxacin or azithromycin alternatives.
Vaccination: deck describes A,C,Y,W conjugate vaccine; pentavalent A,B,C,Y,W; high-risk/exposure indications + adolescents; serogroup B vaccine has short-lived/limited protection in the slide.

Haemophilus influenzae

ID
Pleomorphic G− nonmotile coccobacillus
X + V factors
Virulence
LPS/Lipid A · OMP · capsule · IgA protease
Disease
Otitis · sinusitis · bronchitis · pneumonia · epiglottitis · meningitis · cellulitis · arthritis
Epiglottitis: 4 D's = dysphagia · dysphonia · drooling · distress. Abrupt/rapid, toxic, hot-potato voice, tripod, fiery red edematous epiglottis.
Deck treatment/prevention: otitis/sinusitis: ampicillin, Augmentin, TMP-SMX. Meningitis: 3rd-gen cephalosporin (ceftriaxone/cefotaxime). Hib vaccine.

Bordetella pertussis

Pathway
FHA attaches to cilia → no invasion → pertussis toxin inactivates Gαi → adenylate cyclase ↑ → cAMP ↑ → mucus + lymphocytosis.
Other virulence
Adenylate cyclase · tracheal cytotoxin · small encapsulated G− coccobacillus.
StageKey clue
Catarrhal1–2 wk; mild URI-like symptoms
Paroxysmal2–4 wk; cough spasms, cyanosis/vomiting, whoop, syncope
Convalescent1–2 wk; spasms resolve over 1–3 mo
Lab: very high WBC with ~50% lymphocytosis · NP swab · Regan-Lowe charcoal + horse blood · culture = deck's gold standard · PCR 0–3 wk · serology 2–8 wk. Deck Rx/prevention: erythromycin + acellular pertussis vaccine (slide label “DaPT”).

Moraxella catarrhalis

ID
G− diplococcus · resembles Neisseria · catalase+ · oxidase+ · does not utilize carbohydrates · blood/chocolate agar.
Virulence
Adhesins · A2 surface protein · LOS = key determinant; Lipid A toxic; LOS drives proinflammatory cytokines.
Clinical: oral/URT colonization → otitis media + pneumonia (especially COPD). Most strains β-lactamase producers. Deck lists amox/clav, β-lactamase-stable cephalosporins, macrolides, fluoroquinolones.

Kingella kingae

Pattern
Toddler (6 mo–4 yr) osteoarticular infection / septic arthritis · endocarditis all ages · oropharyngeal colonization · daycare.
ID
G− coccobacillus with tapered ends · pairs/short chains · oxidase+ · pili + capsule + cytotoxin · difficult culture → PCR preferred for joint fluid.
HACEK: deck lists Haemophilus parainfluenzae, Aggregatibacter, Cardiobacterium hominis, Eikenella corrodens, Kingella kingae. Most Kingella isolates are described as β-lactamase negative.

Exam Traps

Meningococcus vs gonococcus: both glucose+; maltose and capsule separate them.
Moraxella: looks like Neisseria but does not use carbohydrates.
H. influenzae: X/V factors, not Neisseria sugar utilization.
Pertussis: toxin disables inhibitory Gαi → cAMP rises. It does not invade.
Kingella: toddler septic arthritis + daycare + difficult culture → think PCR/HACEK.
Vaccine limitation: deck explicitly emphasizes limited/short-lived serogroup B meningococcal protection.
