Adverse Events

Class-wide handout: GI intolerance · rash · some marrow suppression · seizures if infused rapidly · C. difficile risk. Higher-risk examples: anti-staph penicillins → marrow suppression/AKI; 3rd/4th-gen cephalosporins → C. diff; ceftaroline → marrow suppression; imipenem → neurotoxicity.
