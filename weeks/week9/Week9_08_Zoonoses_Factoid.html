Pathogenesis Anchors

Hantavirus
Endothelium → vascular leak + thrombocytopenia → lung vs kidney dominant syndrome.
Brucella
Intracellular BCV → endosome/ER interaction → replication → bloodstream.
RMSF
R. rickettsii infects systemic vascular endothelium → multisystem injury.
Q fever
Small infectious variant / large replicative variant; aerosol exposure drives disease.
Leptospira
Skin defect → bloodstream → lung/liver/kidney; adhesins bind host proteins.
Psittaci
Elementary body infectious; reticulate body replicative.

Faculty-Style Recognition Traps

RMSF serology trap: IFA may be negative early. The deck explicitly says severe spring/summer febrile illness ± rash → TREAT.
Psittacosis clue: pneumonia + many neutrophils but no organisms on sputum Gram stain.
Q fever clue: relative bradycardia + atypical/funny pneumonia after livestock aerosol/dust exposure.
Lepto clue: AKI with unexpectedly low K+ plus conjunctival suffusion/jaundice.
